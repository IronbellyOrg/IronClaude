# Round 1 advocate: variant 5 (the already built observer versus the sweep)

Advocate: adv-r1-v5 (read-only; wrote only this file). Date: 2026-11-14. Sources: diff-analysis.md, variants 1 to 5 (same folder), plus my own re-checks listed in the "Evidence I re-checked" block under section 3.

## 1. Position summary

Variant 5 is right on the one fact that decides the rest: `superclaude observe` is merged and registered (PRs #503 and #514), reads Orca directly, and the sweep and the observer never mention each other. That removes the premise "the observer will read the sweep JSON" that V1 and parts of V4 rest on, and turns the question into "how do two programs built in isolation relate", where V5's answer (keep both, write one contract, change no code until a few owner decisions are made) survives the V1, V3 and V4 arguments for one data source, one trust design and a fleet view, with two qualifications: "keep both" must not mean "never share a parser", and V5's treatment of v2 inputs and of "TUI Panel" is thinner than it looks.

## 2. Steelman of each opposing variant

### 2.1 Variant 1 (#409 observer versus the sweep, written without knowing the observer exists)

**Strongest version.** V1 is the most careful claim check of the #409 body and of the sweep's own contract. Its core argument: two programs that both show a human "what needs me" will drift unless there is one definition of each term and one source of each fact. It also finds two wrong claims in the #409 body (six threads not five; `policy` IS a JSON row key) and one unsupported sentence (the "boundary this issue already states"). Even after V5, every sweep-side finding (F-02, F-05, F-07, F-13, F-18, C-07, C-25, C-34) stands, and V1's instinct that a single written contract is needed is the same conclusion V5 reaches (D1).

**What V1 gets right that V5 must accept.** (1) The reviewer's four asks (summary, links, named steps, open a session) have no source in either program, and the only deterministic sources V1 found (Task spec text read at `probes.py:1199`, `Command:` lines, handoff files) live on the sweep side. So an observer that ever grows those asks will have to read sweep-side facts: V1's "persist the JSON next to the report" is the prerequisite for V5's own option (b). (2) The two programs already disagree in vocabulary (V5 O-07 confirms and adds a third vocabulary).

**Critique with counter-evidence.** V1's premise is false: "No observer code exists" (C-03, "Verified") is refuted by `git ls-tree origin/master src/superclaude/cli/observe/` (14 entries), `main.py:572,574`, and both PRs MERGED (re-checked: 2026-11-11T19:54:57Z and 2026-11-12T12:36:19Z). The observer is not "a future reader of the sweep JSON": a grep for "sweep" in the observer tree on master returns nothing, and a grep for the observer in the sweep tree, design, command, skill and WORKER-CONTRACT returns nothing (both re-run by me). So F-01, F-03 (as an observer requirement), F-04, F-12, F-14, F-15, C-02, C-03, C-21, C-26 and A-01/A-05/A-06/A-07 describe a consumer that does not exist in that form. Its claim tally does not add up (X-009).
