## 1. Position summary

Variant 4 is the only report that tests #423 (v2) against what is really built, and its core findings survive the discovery that the observer exists: "v2" means two things (F1), v2's relay assumes a channel that only Orca workers have (F7), v2 has no trust or data-only rule (F8, F9), and v2 needs one named fact schema and store (F2, F4, F13, F15). V4 is wrong on one premise: it treated the observer as a future reader of the sweep JSON and did not notice the built `superclaude observe` (V5 is right, verified). That error changes the wording of V4's "(a)/(b)" proposals and of its #409 edit 2 (one data source becomes two collectors behind one contract), but it strengthens, not weakens, V4's F13 and F15. Recommended course: keep V4's #423 edits after correcting five lines (section 5), fold V5's D1 relationship note in as the schema-ownership answer, and do nothing irreversible before the owner answers the nine questions (recommendations in section 8).
