## Standards applied (one per criterion, used for every variant)

The 136 agreed verdicts are loose on "presence" criteria (C5, S4, L3, R4, I-dimension all MET for nearly every variant on a listed item or an id cross-reference) and literal on "absolute" criteria (R1 NOT MET for all five; K1 NOT MET for V1, V4 and V5 on a stated fact that was disproved). I used the same two habits.
