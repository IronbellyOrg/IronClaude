## Emerging consensus

All three advocates' Round 2 "Overall recommendation" sections converge on **(a)+(c)**; V2 withdrew (b) (r2-variant-2 "Overall recommendation").

1. Keep the Ruling rule (`QA:254`) word for word. (a), the person resuming in their own session, is the default and the only route for a `reason=dispute` halt.
2. Add (c): the person hand-writes the `T<id>: Ruling:` line into the ledger and the coordinator dispatches a new resume Task (WC-6), which ledger row R consumes with no review (`LEDGER:154`).
3. Spec additions agreed by all three: (i) after an `/sc:implement` Ruling halt the coordinator shows the person `blocked_question`, the halt text, the absolute ledger path and the worktree, and dispatches no Task for that plan until the person has acted, because a WC-6 retry (`SPEC:84`) would start `operator-review` by automation against `QA:283`; (ii) the person writes or resumes only after the worker has settled (`LEDGER:145`, `SPEC:70`); (iii) a hand-written Ruling after a halt is a sanctioned route, and a line written or drafted by the coordinator or any worker is "executor-invented approval" (`QA:249`); (iv) before every resume Task the coordinator runs a read-only check: last line matches `LEDGER:71`, the line is eligible under either clause of `RETRY:498`, and on failure it tells the person and never edits the ledger; (v) fix the stale `qa.md` anchors at `SPEC:67,132,319,334` and extend AC-4.1 with the hand-written-line plus resume-Task check.
4. Added by V3 R2 only (not in V1 R2 or V2 R2 recommendations): the check also refuses `reason=dispute`, and compares the worktree's `HEAD` and `git status --porcelain` with values recorded at the halt; the coordinator dispatches no other writer into that worktree until the resume Task settles. V2 R2 adds: the blocked contract carries the absolute ledger path and worktree.
5. Reject (b) for now; at most a later opt-in that excludes `reason=dispute`.

Claim under test: **the consensus alone gives a safe and working Ruling path** (the person's own words become the Ruling, no automation starts an operator review, and the rest of the plan finishes in Orca).
