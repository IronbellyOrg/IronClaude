## Scope and method

- Decision D2: when `/sc:implement` halts for a Ruling inside an Orca worker, who may give the Ruling?
- Scored artifacts (the original position papers): V1 = `variant-1-original.md`, option (a), keep the rule; V2 = `variant-2-original.md`, option (b), relayed Ruling; V3 = `variant-3-original.md`, option (c), the person writes the ledger line and the coordinator dispatches a resume Task.

- Rubric: the 30-criterion additive binary rubric in `~/.claude/skills/sc-adversarial-protocol/SKILL.md` ("Qualitative Scoring Layer"). Every cell gives CLAIM (the criterion, restated as a yes/no question in the Criterion column), EVIDENCE, and VERDICT: MET (1) or NOT MET (0). There is no partial credit. A verdict with no specific citation is NOT MET.
- Concessions made in the debate rounds count against the variant that made them. The diff-analysis spot-check verdicts (CONFIRMED / OVERSTATED / WRONG) are applied to Correctness.
- Citation key: `V1:N`, `V2:N`, `V3:N` = line N of that variant file; `DA` = `diff-analysis.md`; `R1-Vn`, `R2-Vn` = round files; `INV-NNN` = `invariant-probe.md`; `SPEC:N`, `QA:N`, `LEDGER:N`, `RETRY:N`, `CAPS:N`, `IMPL:N` as in `DA`.
