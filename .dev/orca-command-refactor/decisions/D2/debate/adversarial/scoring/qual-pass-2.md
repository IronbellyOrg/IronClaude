## Method

- Scorer: independent, not an advocate. Pass 2 evaluates in REVERSE order: Variant 3 (option c), Variant 2 (option b), Variant 1 (option a). `qual-pass-1.md` was not read.
- Rubric: the 30-criterion additive binary rubric of `~/.claude/skills/sc-adversarial-protocol/SKILL.md` ("Qualitative Scoring Layer", lines 1666-1741), with Claim-Evidence-Verdict (CEV). No partial credit; a MET verdict needs a specific citation, otherwise NOT MET.
- Object scored: each variant's original paper (`variant-N-original.md`). Evidence used: `diff-analysis.md` (including its 6-row citation spot-check), `rounds/r1-*.md` and `rounds/r2-*.md` (a concession counts against the conceding variant), `invariant-probe.md`, and the cited sources.
- Sources re-read in this pass: `src/superclaude/skills/sc-implement-protocol/refs/qa.md:244-289`, `refs/ledger.md:60-159` and `:195`, `refs/retry.md:486-500`, spec lines 66, 67, 70, 84, 132, 170, 274, 319, 322, 333-337 of `.dev/orca-command-refactor/specs/orca-worker-contract-spec.md`, `src/superclaude/commands/implement.md:55-59`, `.dev/orca-command-refactor/inventory/orca-capabilities.md:58-64`, and `git log -1 -- refs/qa.md` (80f934de, which confirms V1's drift note).
- Citation key: `SPEC:N`, `QA:N`, `LEDGER:N`, `RETRY:N`, `IMPL:N`, `CAPS:N` as in `diff-analysis.md`. `V<n> L<k>` = line k of `variant-<n>-original.md`. `SC-n` = diff-analysis citation spot-check row n. `INV-NNN` = invariant-probe finding.
- Cell format: each evidence cell gives the CLAIM tested, then EVIDENCE. The verdict column gives MET (1) or NOT MET (0).
