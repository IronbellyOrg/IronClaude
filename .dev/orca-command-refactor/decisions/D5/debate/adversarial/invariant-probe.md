## Emerging consensus

Both advocates end Round 2 recommending option (a), keep depth 1; Variant 2 withdraws (b) (R2-V2 Overall recommendation). Agreed points from the two "Final per-point positions" tables:

1. Depth cannot reach a command's internal Agent-tool fan-out; the section 1.3 design stays rejected, and depth fixes only R1 of R1-R5 (C-005, X-003).
2. Depth 1 is the tighter bound; a raise widens it by one generation and applies machine-wide, because the setting lives in the only profile and `worker-start` has no depth flag (C-001, C-002; `CAPS:98`, `CAPS:135`). The word "removes the fence" is wrong; the refusal fires at any maximum (`CAPS:99-100`).
3. Per-unit Task records come from coordinator splitting (C-003, C-010, X-001; `SPEC:42`, `CAPS:158`).
4. The worker-session route to depth 2 exists and avoids R2-R4, but keeps R5, needs a new composite brief type outside the one-command brief (`SPEC:79`), inherits the coordinator loop, would share the coordinator's Run inbox, and saves one coordinator round trip (A-002 = equal; R2-V1 NE1-NE3; R2-V2 NE1-NE2).
5. A-003 resolved for V1 by both: at depth 1 a (presumed) mechanical refusal backs the contract on every run on the machine, whereas under (b) only IronClaude briefs carry a restraint.
6. Remaining disagreement is only on what the D5 text records: both want the scoped form of (b) recorded (depth exactly 2, worker session only, brief line `Sub-workers: allowed, max N`, no command changes) and a revisit trigger tied to a real run with a split knowable only at run time. V2 adds: reword `SPEC:350` and `SPEC:353`; require a defined composite brief before any revisit; verify the refusal call sites, `task-create` at depth 1 and depth-2 mailbox delivery before any raise.

None of the four reverses the choice of option (a): R5, the missing composite brief and machine-wide scope still count against (b). They show that the consensus's stated *reason* (depth 1 is a mechanical backstop on worker fan-out) is not demonstrated, and that option (a) carries an unstated precondition (a root coordinator). Before convergence, the D5 text or the spec needs four things: a stated root-coordinator precondition; a worker-session rule banning `worker-start`, `dispatch` and `task-create`; a read-only P0 trace of the refusal call sites, including `task-create`; and either the backstop claim dropped from D5 or one of these guards supporting it.
