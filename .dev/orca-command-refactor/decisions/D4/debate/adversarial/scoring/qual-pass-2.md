# D4 Qualitative Scoring, Pass 2 (reverse order: V3, V2, V1)

## Method

- Scorer: independent, not an advocate. Pass 2 evaluates in reverse order: Variant 3 (option c), then Variant 2 (option b), then Variant 1 (option a). `qual-pass-1.md` was not read.
- Rubric: the 30-criterion additive binary rubric in `~/.claude/skills/sc-adversarial-protocol/SKILL.md` ("Qualitative Scoring Layer", lines 1666-1741). No partial credit. A MET verdict needs a specific citation; without one the verdict is NOT MET.
- What is scored: the original variant text (`variant-N-original.md`, cited `V3:N`, `V2:N`, `V1:N`). Debate concessions in `rounds/` count against the conceding variant (cited `R1-Vn:N`, `R2-Vn:N`). Diff findings are cited `DA:N` (`diff-analysis.md`), including its citation spot-check (`DA:96-107`). Invariant findings are cited `INV-NNN` (`invariant-probe.md`); most target the Round 2 consensus (c′), not the originals, so they are used only where they bear on an original variant's text.
- Each table cell gives CLAIM (met or not met in that variant), then EVIDENCE (quote or section, or "No evidence found, searched: ..."). The verdict column gives MET (1) or NOT MET (0).
- Sources re-read for this pass on 2026-11-03, branch `ironbelly/OrcaCLICommandRefactor-Specs`: `HOW-WE-WORK.md:212`, `:282`, `:324`; `sc-post-release-protocol/SKILL.md:333`, `:340`, `:347`, `:351`; `sc-implement-protocol/refs/retry.md:41`, `:44`; `CLAUDE.md:37`, `:50`, `:68`; spec `orca-worker-contract-spec.md:73`, `:75`, `:156`, `:275`, `:333-348`; `issue-326-evaluation.md:17-19`, `:50-51`. All quotes the variants use from these lines match. The spot-check's two OVERSTATED rows (`CLAUDE.md:68` covers upstream contributions only; `PRL:351` says "this session") were confirmed on re-read.
- Position-file convention (Structure 5) was taken from the sibling option files: every D1-D5 option file has the four sections Safety, Reliability, How a person works day to day and Implementation cost. Options (b) add "Strongest case for it", and the third options (D1, D3, D4 option-c) add "Why a third option".
