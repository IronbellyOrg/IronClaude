- **OQ-7.2 (new at CP3, non-gate-blocking)** — `tests/swarm/test_uv_enforcement.py::test_no_forbidden_python_or_pip_invocations[python -m-...]` now flags `src/superclaude/cli/swarm/commands.py:782` for the literal substring `python -m superclaude.cli.main swarm`.
