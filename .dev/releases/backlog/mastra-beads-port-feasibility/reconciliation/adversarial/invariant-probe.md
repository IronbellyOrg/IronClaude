### Sufficiency Challenge Answer

Running Phase 0 as a two-week intelligence sprint is necessary and valuable, but it is not sufficient to decide that the port will succeed. It can decide whether to continue investigating or stop early, but a "go" after Phase 0 should mean "authorize the next bounded validation phase," not "the replatforming is feasible end-to-end." Downstream conditions still stand between Phase 0 and success: the permanent-polyglot staffing/ownership model must be real, the broader Python-behind-MCP boundary must perform under representative convergence/audit/FMEA load, Backlog must prove lossless round-trip before task-of-record promotion, and the multi-tenant control plane must pass isolation/fairness/pilot-throughput gates.

The three new gates G-A/G-B/G-C are necessary but not sufficient. The merged recommendation should add at least two more gates: (1) an operating-model gate for durable Python+Node+MCP+HTTP ownership, staffing, on-call, and version maintenance; and (2) an end-to-end pilot/control-plane gate that proves tenant isolation, noisy-neighbor protection, tool-portfolio coverage, and throughput advantage over the current CLI + worktrees/subagents baseline.
