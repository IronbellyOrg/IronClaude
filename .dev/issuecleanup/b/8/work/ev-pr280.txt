### Necessary deviation: verify-deps audits a separate env

With the activated venv, `uv pip install -e ".[dev]"` ignores `uv.lock` and resolves `py-cpuinfo2`, which is not in `scripts/dependency_baseline.txt`. That turns `make verify-deps` red. The step now sets `UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT: ${{ runner.temp }}/verify-deps-venv`, so `uv run` builds a separate `uv.lock`-resolved env, exactly what master audits today. Reproduced locally; see `t6-setup-uv-docs.md` in the plan package.
