# #716 [FEATURE] Coordinator: next release, the features of issue 655 that our current plan does not cover [OPEN]
## What was tried
Issue 655 (Clay's design for an issue-polling orchestrator) was split into its 14 sections of the Proposed Solution, and one analyst per section classified every design feature against our current plan (the unification spec and its decision record). Result over all sections: 488 features, 248 already covered by the current plan, 124 in direct conflict (the current plan wins and that part of 655 is rejected), 116 not covered. The not-covered features were sorted into three groups: the coordinator, the watcher and the TUI dashboard. This issue holds the group for the per-project coordinator session and skill (`/sc:coordinator`, session `COORD-<RepoName>`): 82 classified rows, merged into 27 items below. Of the 116 not-covered rows, 115 appear in the three issues; one row (S10-F60, the ordering step 'wait for additions A to E', already done in Clay's own spec work) was left out because its analyst judged it not a product feature. Rows that said the same thing in several sections of 655 were merged into one item and keep their row ids. Wherever the current plan decided otherwise, the plan was kept and nothing from 655 is requested here. Dashes inside quotes were replaced with hyphens. The classification was done by agents; every `UNVERIFIED` mark in an item is theirs and still open.
