### C13. Orca decision gates as a way to reach the person, with answer-check (655 section 2, 4, 6; rows S02-F36, S04-F36, S06-F5)
  - What to build: The coordinator may open an Orca decision gate to park a task while it waits for a person, but a gate can never itself carry an approval.
  - Also in this item:
    - S04-F36: An escalation can open an Orca decision gate for the person, and the coordinator treats the gate's resolution as an answer only after `answer-check`.
    - S06-F5: When the coordinator writes an escalation, it also opens an Orca gate for it so the pending question is visible to Orca and the sweep, and closes the gate when the recorded answer arrives.
  - Done when: a test shows `gate-resolve` alone does not satisfy `answer-check` or a merge go; only a recorded chat go does.
    - also: A throwaway escalation opens a gate; resolving it in Orca is accepted as an answer only if `answer-check` confirms a person typed it for the right question and head.
    - also: A test escalation produces one open gate that `orca orchestration` lists, the sweep report shows a pending question for it, and the gate is closed after the typed answer passes `answer-check`.
  - Research and debate results from our documents:
    - S02-F36: .dev/brainstorms/20261120-pr686-unify/spec/SPEC.md:297 `Accept a recorded go only with the owner's quoted words ... typed by a person in their own session, never relayed`; RK-3 `answer-check cannot tell a person from a script` (SPEC.md:647).
    - S04-F36: SPEC:297 (SEC-08) "typed by a person in their own session, never relayed"; D15:104 (D20) "An answer is always typed".
    - S06-F5: TRO:1225 "Sweep coverage gaps: ... `Messages coverage: partial` ... pending questions cannot be read" (the sweep cannot see questions today). TRO:972-976 shows the sweep and observer allowlists contain no gate verb.
  - Watch out:
    - S02-F36: owner rule: every approval is given in chat and never in a file or another channel (SPEC.md:303). Resolving a gate in Orca could be script-typed and would not meet SEC-08 as a go.
    - S04-F36: a gate resolved in the Orca interface may not satisfy SEC-08 (typed in live chat, never relayed); UNVERIFIED how Orca records who resolved a gate. The owner's rule "approvals through chat, never files" also applies. Treat the gate as a display channel until the owner rules.
