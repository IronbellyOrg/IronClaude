PR #608 [MERGED 2026-11-18T03:34:22Z] docs(style): define plain English and give the wrap-up a fixed five-part body
## What changed and why

Clay, William, Leandro and Ryan said that the replies agents write are hard to understand. This change to the Pragmatic output style (`src/superclaude/output-styles/pragmatic.md`) does four things:

1. It says what "plain English" means, and requires every named thing (an issue number, a tool, a branch) to be introduced with what it is, plus its link or path.
2. It requires every question or approval request to be followed by one line per possible answer saying what that answer would do.
3. It adds a before-sending check: could someone who has not seen this work follow it, is every number or name explained, does every question have its answer lines, is every sentence needed.
4. It gives the wrap-up a fixed body after the table: **What I did**, **How**, **Where it stands**, **What's next**, **My recommendation**.

The DONE / NEXT table is not changed in any way: same two columns (Status, Result), same row style. Line 22 and the sweep exception are untouched. The long-reply limit (line 29) is untouched, so this does not invite longer replies.
