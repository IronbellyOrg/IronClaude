## Recommendations and roadmap
1. Keep the feature open as **in-progress**, and align its current acceptance baseline with programme issue #549 before implementation.
2. Reuse the existing resolver, findings parsers, ledger and telemetry. Specify the small caller adapter boundary and the mapping between findings-v1 and findings-v2, including consequence-based blocking rules introduced by merged #582.
3. Complete the planned reflect rollout beyond shadow only after its exit gates and caller-contract compatibility are proved; do not silently flip its default. Test workflow through reflect with preserved `reflect_*` recording and the separate Wave 4R behavior.
4. Keep spec-panel's existing lifecycle tests, add cross-caller checks and an end-to-end revision/re-review demonstration, and document cli-eval as indirect and cli-portify as retired. No second panel is needed.
