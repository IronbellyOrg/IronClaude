# Research: orchestrator mode Phase 1 (read-only `/sc:sweep` and the WC-14 coordinator-loop rule), issue #339

## Task Overview

Issue #339 asks for an orchestrator mode in which a coordinator session can see, at a glance, which of its Orca workers and ccsession sessions are waiting on a person, working, stalled, finished or idle, and later close or resume them. This task researches Phase 1 only: a read-only, coordinator-only `/sc:sweep` that classifies sessions into the classes waiting on a person, working, stalled (proved), could not determine, finished and idle, with safe-to-close as a separate dimension, over a default 6 hour window that bounds activity only, and writes a dated static report on the #409 Shift Brief and Attention Ledger layout; plus a WC-14 coordinator-loop rule in `src/superclaude/core/WORKER-CONTRACT.md`. Later phases (`/sc:close`, resume, the trust layer, an orchestrator of orchestrators and the management-server relay) are recorded as follow-ups and not designed here.

The research workspace already holds `research/research-notes.md` and four scope files (contract, Orca and crash recovery, CLI patterns, #409 layout) written by the tech-research skill. This task verifies those files against the current code, fills the two Critical gaps G-A (what a `worker-list` row carries) and G-B (the Orca open questions on ask/reply, peer sessions and frozen workers), adds two external-pattern files, runs a full-intensity research gate, synthesizes six section files, runs a full-intensity synthesis gate, assembles `RESEARCH-REPORT-orchestrator-mode.md`, and validates it through 11 lens agents, 4 source-fidelity agents plus 1 cross-source contradictions agent, and 4 anti-omission agents. Every source is read-only: files, git history, `--help` text and existing captures. No item deploys, installs, launches an Orca or ccsession worker, runs a real sweep or close, changes user or global settings, or edits a file outside this task folder.
