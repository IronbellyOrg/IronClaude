# Auggie Claim Gate implementation

Source: ./source.md
Goal: auggie-review publishes, counts, posts and offers remediation only for admitted defects, decided by one evidence-based admission step and one checked ledger, while unproven, rejected, intentional-limitation and documented-prerequisite candidates stay recorded locally.
