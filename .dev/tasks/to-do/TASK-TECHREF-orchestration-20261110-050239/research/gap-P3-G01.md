## Key takeaways

1. The worker-side half of the lifecycle is source-readable and is now traced. The preamble tells a worker to send `worker_done` exactly once with `--outcome succeeded|failed`, to `ask` with `--timeout-ms 600000` and resume by message ID, to `check --terminal` and `--ack`, to heartbeat only when the setting is on (default on, 5 minutes, range 1-60, resolved per dispatch), and, when depth allows, how to start helpers (which `WC-13` forbids for IronClaude workers).
2. Orca gives the coordinator no stall verdict. It gives fleet liveness (`exited` durable proof, `unverifiable` absence), PTY liveness, `agentWait`, and a raw `lastHeartbeatAt`. `unverifiable` is always absence of positive proof, never proof of death (Orca's guide, `bundled-skill-guides.ts:4504-4509`); it carries one of ten reasons (S5a). For a terminal worker the reasons are no agent-hook row, a restored row, a clock problem or no agent-hook activity for 30 minutes; for a local chat-session worker the reason is `host_unavailable`; remote reasons appear only with `--include-remote`. It is not a measured silence of the worker and not the 60 second window of the attempt projection.
3. The attempt-outcome projection (60 s freshness, `finished_unverified`) has no production writer for its liveness, process-turn and outcome facts, in CHECKOUT and INSTALLED-FILES. Nothing should be documented as relying on `finished_unverified`.
4. "Orca does not start ready Tasks itself" holds for the live surface: the automatic scheduler is retired by CLI handlers and an RPC admission fence, and a dormant copy remains in source and in the installed bundle.
5. The installed build files match the checkout on every point traced, except the nested-depth default (already recorded: 1 in the checkout, 2 installed).
6. `WORKER-CONTRACT.md` mirrors Orca's own coordinator guidance on stall proofs (`exited`, process exit, final transcript turn without `worker_done`) and deliberately overrides its `--retry-of` advice (WC-6).
