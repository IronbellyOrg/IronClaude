## How to read this file

- **Source key.** `NN` = `research/NN-*.md` (01 finding-contract, 02 ledger-merge-verdict, 03 clustering, 04 panel-resolution-identity, 05 blocking-calibration, 06 input-contract-briefs, 07 reflect-merge-contract, 08 run-recording-telemetry, 09 consumer-plugpoints, 10 future-consumers, 11 evaluation-evidence, 12 test-pins-regression, 13 gap-fill-reflect-ensemble). `RN` = `research/research-notes.md`. `CV1`/`CV2` = `qa/analyst-cross-validation-report-1.md`/`-2.md`. `GR1`/`GR2` = `qa/qa-research-gaps-report-1.md`/`-2.md`. `QC` = `qa/qa-consolidated-findings-phase3.md`. `QX` = `qa/qa-verification-phase3-extra-fix.md`. `QF` = `qa/qa-fix-log-phase3.md`. `Gn` = numbered entry n of that file's Gaps and Questions section; unnumbered bullets are cited by topic; `Q` = a "Question" or "Open" bullet.
- **Severity.** Critical = blocks a required report part; Important = changes a conclusion, a conflict-log row or a design decision if left unanswered; Minor = a note. Where a QA report rated the gap, that rating is used and named in brackets (the higher rating wins when two lenses differ). Gaps without a bracket were unrated by QA and are rated here by the same rule. No gap is Critical: both cross-validation reports and both gap-detection reports state that no gap blocks synthesis.
- **Effort (E1/E2/E3).** The studied effort the gap belongs to, taken from the effort tags in the source research files: E1 = the reflect panel on `ironbelly/shadow-runs` (kernel, shadow mode, `sc-reflect-protocol`, `cli/reflect`); E2 = the tasklist review-panel study at E2 commit 4a22d74213f93837d00c2c314075dd967b9a98e3; E3 = the #346 review loop on master (`cli/review` ledger, merge, telemetry, spec-panel loop). A combination such as E1+E3 means the gap spans efforts. `cross` = no single effort applies (future consumers such as PR review and rf-qa, and notes about the task itself).
- **Closure effort (C1/C2/C3).** The work needed to close the gap. C1 = no new work: the report can close or carry it from existing evidence (a citation, a wording fix, or a recommendation the evidence supports). C2 = a small read-only check (one file read, a grep, a sample). C3 = new research, a measured run, ground truth, or an input only the owner or another session can supply.
- **Type.** unverified claim / code-contradicted doc / missing evidence / owner decision / pending external input. "Owner decision" covers every Question or Open bullet that the evidence cannot settle by itself.
- **Affects.** (a) overlap matrix, (b) value inventory, (c) conflict log, (d) target architecture, (e) future-use exposure list. Section 8 = migration path and no-regression gates, Section 9 = Open Questions, Section 10 = evidence trail.
- **Deduplication.** One row per underlying gap; rows merge entries that several files or both QA lenses repeat. Items already answered by a sibling file or a QA check are not carried as open; they are listed in section 4 with the answer. Defects found by the research (for example the ten ensemble defects in `research/13` section 10) are findings for the conflict log, not gaps, and are not repeated here.
