## The topic to brainstorm

Write the SPEC (requirements and design options, no code) for a framework-wide revision that loosens the classification of blockers. Review gates must keep finding real blocking issues, but findings that change no decision must be RECORDED, and the user must be OFFERED to resolve them or file them as follow-up issues, instead of halting everything and forcing fix cycles and re-reviews.

Gate 2: the 15 reviewers' own blocking counts summed to 23; the consolidation counted 8 after the override and de-duplication. Gate 1 cost an extra fix cycle and a third review (about 25 minutes) for two formatting defects.
