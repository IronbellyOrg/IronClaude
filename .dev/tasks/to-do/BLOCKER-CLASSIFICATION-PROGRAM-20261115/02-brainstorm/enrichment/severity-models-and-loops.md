## 2. /sc:spec-panel severity (base, variants, stoprule skill, grader)

Source of the base command: `src/superclaude/commands/spec-panel.md`. Variants exist only in `~/.claude/commands/sc/spec-panel-{armb,diffgate,ledger,ponytail,stoprule}.md` (section 0).

Base command (spec-panel.md):
- Scale CRITICAL/MAJOR/MINOR per finding (spec-panel.md:123). Review Loop bullet 2 (spec-panel.md:259): "grade by consequence, not by label; CRITICAL is blocking; a MAJOR or an FR-8/FR-9 finding is blocking only if it names a concrete wrong outcome an implementer would produce or a security, data-loss or safety-boundary risk, otherwise it is Medium (a warning listed under `follow-up candidates`); MINOR is a note; Medium and minor findings never trigger a revision, never fail a review and never appear in the Reviser brief."
- FR-8/FR-9 (spec-panel.md:332-333): GAP cell / blank behavior is blocking only on the same test, else Medium. A dimensional mismatch is always CRITICAL (spec-panel.md:372).
- Fail: `verdict: FAIL` only when a blocking finding is open (spec-panel.md:260). Cap: "at most 2 fix cycles (3 reviews), then escalate to the user with the open blocking findings", header `review: N of 3` (spec-panel.md:261). Reviser contract and precedence at spec-panel.md:263-264.
- Who assigns: the reviewer labels; the coordinator "grades by consequence" (prose). No code in the base command.
- Recorded class: YES. Medium -> `follow-up candidates`; "asks the user once per loop whether to open an issue ... creates none without the user's answer; an `--unattended` run only records the list" (spec-panel.md:260).
- Maps onto SR: nearly identical in spirit ("concrete wrong outcome an implementer would produce" = "a decision the deliverable rests on is wrong"). The clause "CRITICAL is blocking" without the wrong-outcome test, and the always-CRITICAL dimensional mismatch (:372), are broader than SR. Stoprule limits CRITICAL to kind (e) or a requirement the spec calls core (rubric.md:10).

Variants (installed only), grading line:
- armb `spec-panel-armb.md:257` and ponytail `spec-panel-ponytail.md:258`: "CRITICAL and FR-8/FR-9 findings are blocking; MINOR is minor; a MAJOR is blocking only if it names a concrete wrong outcome ...". No Medium class; FR-8/FR-9 always blocking (older text).
- diffgate `spec-panel-diffgate.md:257`: same, plus "the script derives blocking from each `FINDING` line (`sev=critical`, `fr=8` or `fr=9`, or `sev=major` with an `outcome` other than `-`) and the reviewer never writes it." Derive-by-script.
- ledger `spec-panel-ledger.md:259`: the reviewer writes `grade` (CRITICAL/MAJOR/MINOR), `rule`, `wrong_outcome` and an exact `anchor` in a `ledger-input` block; "the script, not this session, decides which rows block." Cap at `spec-panel-ledger.md:263`.
- stoprule `spec-panel-stoprule.md:335-336`: GAP/blank rows are "blocking only under rubric B1-B3 of the Review Loop, otherwise minor"; precedence at `:270` adds "may not change the rubric or its hash".

Stoprule skill (`~/.claude/skills/sc-spec-panel-stoprule-protocol/`):
- Rubric v1, frozen (`refs/rubric.md:1-15`). BLOCKING needs all of B1, B2 and B3:
  - B1 Quote: a verbatim substring of the spec under review plus the defective span; for an omission, quote the requirement whose promised behavior is incomplete (rubric.md:3-4).
  - B2 Outcome: a concrete wrong outcome: a named reader/implementer/test following the quoted text exactly gets a specific wrong result; or two quoted texts cannot both hold; or a check that cannot pass or always passes, with the input that shows it (rubric.md:5-6).
  - B3 Kind, one of: (a) contradiction between two quoted texts; (b) a claim about a source that the source's quoted text contradicts; (c) an acceptance check that cannot pass or passes vacuously; (d) one text with two readings leading to different observable results; (e) a step that loses data or takes an unsafe action (rubric.md:7-9).
  - CRITICAL is blocking only under (e) or in a requirement the spec calls core (rubric.md:10). MINOR is everything else, including "wording, missing example, moved line number or citation drift, style, a gap whose intended behaviour is inferable from nearby text, a suggestion to add a rule, mechanism or section" (rubric.md:11-12). Never a reason to be blocking: experts agree or disagree, the section is important, the finding was found late, a GAP/blank Boundary Table row (rubric.md:13-14). Q2 in re-reviews is recorded "never demotes" (rubric.md:15).
- What `scripts/stoprule.py` computes (stdlib only; exit codes 0 ok, 3 repairable once, 4 HALT at cap, 5 stop on bad input, 6 ledger tampered, stoprule.py:8, 23): a sealed JSON-lines claim ledger inside the report with a hash chain (stoprule.py:10-12); deterministic grade batches of at most 6 claims plus one blocking and one minor canary (`build_batches` :657); grader reply parsing (:789); per-claim status `confirmed|minor|late-found|minor-form|unconfirmed|confirmed-audit|pending` derived from the ledger only (`claim_statuses` :913-949: not form-ok -> minor-form; late -> late-found; grade blocking -> confirmed, else minor); frozen prior status (:902-910); and the verdict as a pure function (`compute_verdict` :1162-1205): FAIL/confirmed if any confirmed defect group or audit hunk; FAIL/unresolved-prior; FAIL/incomplete if any claim waiting, any uncovered hunk, no seal, or no valid PASS-time audit ("silence never passes", :1185-1189); else PASS. It also renders the Reviser brief (:1358), the escalation section at the cap (:1407, :1528) and the sentinel plus last `verdict:` line (:1538-1543). `CAP_SENTENCE` is the same 2-fix-cycle text (:1153).
- Grader `~/.claude/agents/finding-grader.md:12-22, 24-35`: blind (no spec, no report, no file reads), model sonnet, one JSON line per claim `{"cid","blocking","q2","why"}`; "A claim is blocking only when it meets rubric B1, B2 and B3"; `verified` evidence is treated as true, `unverified` is graded as if true with a note in `why`.
- Skill steps S0-S10: `SKILL.md:19-30`. Grader reply invalid twice -> claims stay `unconfirmed`, verdict FAIL `incomplete` (`SKILL.md:52`). Exit 4 = HALT at cap (`SKILL.md:55`).
- Recorded class: YES. The tail has "Not blocking (listed, never looped on)" (stoprule.py:1506).
- Maps onto SR: B2 with B3(a)-(d) is a spec-review form of "a decision/number the deliverable rests on is wrong" (it demands a concrete wrong outcome); B3(e) = security/data-loss/safety. "Required deliverable missing" has no rubric slot except an omission quote. Whether spec review needs that clause is UNVERIFIED. This is the strongest derive-by-code design: the blocking decision is taken out of the reviewer's hands and the verdict is a pure function of the ledger.

## 3. card_convert.py severity mapping

- Purpose (docstring :1-47): converts a recorded reflect reviewer card to findings-v2 lines with a deterministic, quote-free rule; reports a conversion share. Card shapes `yaml-blocking-minor`, `yaml-other-severity`, `markdown-table`, `unlisted` (:5-10, :65).
- Severity words (docstring :36-40; code :71-83 and :193-198): `blocking, blocker, major, high, must-fix, p1` -> MAJOR + blocking True; `critical, p0` -> CRITICAL + blocking True; `minor, medium, low, should-fix, important, nit, info, p2, p3` -> MINOR + blocking False (case-insensitive); any other word -> severity None and blocking None (:197-198). Note `important` and `medium` map to non-blocking here, while rf-qa treats IMPORTANT as blocking (`rf-qa.md:111`).

- Consumers (grep over src, scripts, tests):

## 4. Reflect deviation taxonomy

Files: `src/superclaude/skills/sc-reflect-protocol/refs/deviation-taxonomy.md`, `src/superclaude/skills/sc-reflect-protocol/SKILL.md`, `src/superclaude/agents/reflect-reviewer.md`.

- Four classes (taxonomy:5; definitions :26-84): Authorized (explicitly approved scope addition; default remediation none, :38), Necessary (forced by a documented constraint, not pre-authorized; "Documentation note", :54), Drift (silent change with no rationale and no contradiction; "Authorize-or-revert decision required", :70), Regression (contradicts an acceptance criterion, an explicit spec constraint or a previously passing test; unconditional Tier 3 offer and forced Tier 2 escalation, :84). Precedence Regression > Drift > Necessary > Authorized (:90). No 5th class: evidence-insufficient items go to `grounding-gaps.yaml` (:129-154); no-spec correctness gaps go to advisory `correctness-gaps.yaml` (:156-180).
- Relation to blocking: the taxonomy has no "blocking" word. Blocking is applied at the promotion gate (SKILL.md:1540, condition 4): "`deviation_count_by_class.drift == 0` AND `deviation_count_by_class.regression == 0` — Authorized expansion and Necessary deviation are non-blocking; Drift and Regression block." Condition 6 also needs `citations_dropped == 0` and empty grounding gaps (SKILL.md:1544); non-empty grounding gaps force `status: partial` and `needs_human_decision` (taxonomy:148-152). The correctness-gap channel is advisory and gates nothing (taxonomy:162). Advisory Reuse-Miss findings "do NOT increment these counters and do NOT gate" (SKILL.md:1540).
- Separate severity scale: the reflect-reviewer table uses HIGH (regression or load-bearing-invariant break) / MEDIUM (unjustified drift with real impact) / LOW (cosmetic or minor drift) (`reflect-reviewer.md:71`, table :84-87). The reviewer assigns both the class and the word; the gate counts classes, not the word.
- Findings-v2 side: `deviation_class` in {authorized, necessary, drift, regression, none} (`findings_v2.py:38`); `reflect_category` `deviation` needs a class (`:163-164`); card_convert reads `deviation_class` (`card_convert.py:42, 70`).
- Non-blocking-but-recorded: YES. Authorized and Necessary appear in the report and ledger without gating; correctness gaps are advisory.
- Maps onto SR: Regression = "contradicts an acceptance criterion" = a required deliverable or decision broken; fits SR. Drift is the mismatch: today every Drift blocks promotion (condition 4) and defaults to "Authorize-or-revert decision required". Under SR, a Drift with no decision resting on it and no safety risk would be a follow-up. That is where SR would change reflect. UNVERIFIED how many real runs would flip.
