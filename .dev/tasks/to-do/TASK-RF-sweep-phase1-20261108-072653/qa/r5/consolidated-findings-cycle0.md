### R5-F1 (MAJOR): the claim "not called in Phase 1" for the two `--agent-wait` templates has no test; only `worktree list` and `worktree current` do
- Sources: AC A1 (MAJOR, kept). Merged duplicates: IC F1 (MINOR), NM N5 (MINOR), CR F1 (MINOR), DA F3 (MINOR), SF F-3 is a separate defect and is kept as R5-F23.
- Evidence: `docs/designs/orchestrator-mode.md:535-536` mark templates 9 and 10 "not called in Phase 1"; `:679` (WC-16) and `:833` (D6) tell the reader `/sc:sweep` does not run the listing; `:895` says step 1.0a runs only templates 1 to 8. The "zero argv" assertion exists only for `worktree list` and `worktree current` (`:555` and the `test_probes_allowlist.py` row at `:759`).
- Effect: a `probes.py` developer can add a `worktree ps --agent-wait` call and every listed test stays green, so the WC-16 claim that `/sc:sweep` does not run the listing is unenforced.
- Smallest fix: extend the zero-argv assertion at `:555` and in the `:759` row to cover both `worktree ps --agent-wait` templates.
