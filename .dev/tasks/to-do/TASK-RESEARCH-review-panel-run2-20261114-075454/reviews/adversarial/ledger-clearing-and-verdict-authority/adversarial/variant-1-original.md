## 1. The position in one paragraph

Position A keeps the shipped shape of the multi-reviewer ledger (`review-ledger/1`, one merge pass per round, a row cleared in the round after its fix) and closes the reproduced holes inside it instead of replacing its semantics. (a) Silence stops being evidence: a row in `resolved_check` clears only when the round has a quorum of reviewers whose recorded completion state is `complete` and at least that quorum typed a `prior ... resolved` line for the row (the examination record); a `prior open|regressed` line from any complete reviewer withholds clearing. (b) The reviewer keeps typing `blocking`; no second authority (script predicate, blind grader) is added to the core, and the `verdict:` line stays parser-neutral dead input. (c) The verdict, decision and `brief_ids` are a pure function of rows, the last round number and the cap, computed on every read and never stored. (d) `defer` takes an owner and an expiry, may only park an open blocking row, never edits a round record, keeps the row counted as open in the verdict, and returns the row to `open` when a blocking re-find arrives or the expiry round is reached. The quorum value Q and the DEGRADED-style outcome for a partial panel belong to slot C-03; A needs only a predicate "Q complete reviewers" and takes Q as a parameter.

## 7. The strongest objection to A, and the answer

Objection. The examination record is a self-report. A `prior L1 resolved` line costs a reviewer as little as silence costs today; a reviewer that did not read the revised region types it as readily. A therefore moves the failure from "silence clears" to "an unverified affirmation clears". Position B's verification is checkable: a per-row answer whose evidence quote must occur in the new snapshot (`~/.claude/skills/sc-review-ledger/SKILL.md:60`, `review_ledger.py:1829-1838`), and an unanswered row is open or ESCALATE (`review_ledger.py:1903-1907`, `SKILL.md:84`). Two measured facts point the same way: reviewers did not reference the driving document in 156 of 665 E2 runs (research/08 G6), and the only measure of reviewer-typed blocking against ground truth is low: "real blocking" 0/8 in every pilot arm and 0.17 for the default panel (research/08 table lines 26 and 124). So both A's examination record and A's typed `blocking` rest on reviewer honesty and reviewer accuracy that are unproven.

Answer.
1. A does not claim the record proves examination. It removes three reproduced paths that need no dishonesty at all: an unrelated empty block clears a row found by 3 of 3 reviewers; a reviewer with an invalid block clears rows (the docstring at `ledger.py:112` says it must not); a requested reviewer that returned nothing counts for nothing. After A every cleared row carries a positive, attributable statement by a named complete reviewer with a recorded report hash (`ledger.py:146`); today a cleared row carries no attributable evidence. The remaining residue (a false affirmation) is smaller than the removed set and visible in the history.
2. The 156 of 665 figure measures non-use of `source.md` under a brief that never named it (the loop and E2 briefs name only the candidate, B-1, decided at C-S8); its cause is [UNVERIFIED] (C-S15, COMBO-REPORT:373 calls it an inference). It says nothing about the honesty of a `prior` line, a field the shipped brief already requests row by row. The "real blocking" figures count how many ground-truth implement-blocking items appeared in a row the reviewer typed as blocking, under one authority only; they do not compare authorities. G4 (65 judge-blocking against 38 real) measures the E2 judge, not reviewer typing (research/08 G4). No run compares the typed authority with a predicate or a grader (`research/14:76, 217`).
3. Quote-verified answers cost one answer per live row per round and a locate against the new snapshot; their benefit in a panel is unmeasured because the variants are single-reviewer loops with no valid repeated result (`research/03` section 3.2; `research/14:217`). Whether A's record needs a checkable quote is the one question the section 5 measurement answers. If false affirmations turn out frequent there, A's guard has the wrong strength and verification of the quoted kind is the right one; if compliance and false-clear rates are acceptable, A delivers the closure at no added model call, with the shipped grammar, the shipped prose and the shipped pins intact.
