**Shipped today.** The two uses of the word are two scales for two jobs. In the skill, High and Medium are the two defect labels the patch loop must fix and Low is a note. In the CLI gate, HIGH alone decides exit 1 and MEDIUM is a counted report field.

**What the debate did not settle (C-001): whether the skill's Medium blocks.** Final positions:

B reads A+'s High clause and H+'s Medium as the same blocking boundary and locates the differences in the label word, a buffer tier, churn and the PR:62 edit (sections 3.3 and 10).
