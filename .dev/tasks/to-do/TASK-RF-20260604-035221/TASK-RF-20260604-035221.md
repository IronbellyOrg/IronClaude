## Open Questions

**OQ-1 (Signal B design decision — gated as a `needs_human_decision` item in Phase 3, Step 3.7):** The integrity gate's Signal B (`signal_b_pass`, integrity.py) re-derives the last_completed task's status from its transcript via `_classify_transcript` (imported from `rerun_tasks`). `_classify_transcript` is typed `-> TaskStatus` and **never emits `PASS_RECOVERED`** — a genuinely-recovered task (which by definition exited non-zero with an error result-event) is re-derived as `FAIL_TERMINAL` (or `FAIL_RECOVERABLE`). So even after the Signal A `is_success` widening, Signal B independently fails a recovered seam. This is a DESIGN DECISION, not a one-line swap. Two options must be presented to the user; DO NOT auto-apply a default:
- **Opt-1 (minimal):** Widen only the identity comparisons to `is_success` for consistency/future-proofing, and ACCEPT that the integrity gate still will not validate a recovered seam via Signal B. The load-bearing RED→GREEN signal is the planner-level pair of assertions: (a) the recovered task is NOT in `rerun_task_ids`, and (b) it IS selected as `last_completed`. This is a planner-level fix only.
- **Opt-2 (deeper):** Additionally teach Signal B / `_classify_transcript` to recognize a recovered tail (e.g., treat a `PASS_RECOVERED` persisted status as authoritative, or exempt a recovered `last_completed` from transcript re-derivation). Broader change with its own tests.

The planner sites (`rerun_task_ids` / `last_completed` / `next_unfinished`) and the drift site ARE safe one-line widenings and proceed unconditionally. ONLY integrity Signal B carries the design question.
