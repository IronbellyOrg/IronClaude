+### WC-16. Coordinator loop
+
+The coordinator keeps watching its Run until every Task it created is settled and every result has been inspected and its admission or rejection recorded. It never stops watching on its own. When a wait on Orca expires, the coordinator re-arms it. On every coordinator wake, at most once every 5 minutes, the coordinator runs "The sweep." under `### Liveness supervision` unchanged and, in the same pass, runs `/sc:sweep` and reads its report before it acts. `/sc:sweep` is an added read: it does not replace that sweep, it is not a second pass, and it does not update heartbeat records or run the `worktree ps --agent-wait` listing. Its own reads are not L1 probes and do not count against the L1 cap; the coordinator's own L1 probes stay limited to workers a trigger fired for. The coordinator keeps that pass scheduled while work remains. Phase 1 adds this duty, not a scheduler. The `/sc:sweep` classes follow WC-10: only Orca's stall proofs make a worker stalled; anything else it cannot prove is could not determine. A missing heartbeat is absence, never death. The coordinator escalates to the person, and does not act alone, on: a failed or blocked result it cannot retry under WC-6, a partial result it judges unusable, a third brief-guard refusal, a ready Task it did not create, needing ownership review, a frozen plan, and a fan-in with fewer than 2 admitted inputs. Person-started sessions appear in the report, observed from transcripts and ccsession files; they are never messaged. The `/sc:sweep` report is a dated local file; it is never posted. `/sc:sweep` recommends nothing: no stop, retry, release, merge or removal. The coordinator decides and performs any action under those rules. The coordinator records each admission or rejection in its run notes; `/sc:sweep` does not observe them.
