# Round 1 advocate statement: Variant 3 (close Coder#477 as superseded, two reopen conditions)

Persona: analyzer (evidence quality and logical consistency). Depth quick, Round 1 only.
Sources read: `../question.md`, the three variants, `diff-analysis.md`, ADJ Part 2.5 (lines 278-287), Part 2.7 (lines 301-308), Part 6.3 (lines 601-673), `merged-requirements.md` lines 353-356 and 520-562, `docs/designs/issue-watcher.md` lines 25-60, and `gh issue view 477 --repo IronbellyOrg/Coder` (read-only, 2026-11-14; state OPEN, `updatedAt` 2026-11-13T16:20:07Z, one comment).

## Position summary

Close Coder#477 as superseded. Its whole body exists to serve one consumer, the issue watcher, and that consumer was built without a listener. The program's own recommended answers (Q4 "single-host", Q18 "no TUIBBS channel yet") meet the condition the review gives for closing. Narrowing it rewrites every section and leaves an open issue that no one waits on. Closing with reopen conditions keeps the real future need recorded and costs the fewest owner decisions now.

It keeps the route disabled until the #339 grant format exists (ADJ line 655).

## Strengths claimed

1. **The issue's only stated consumer is the watcher, and the watcher needs no listener.** The issue body's Problem Statement: "The owner describes a sibling IronClaude watcher that needs to reach the intended workspace and hand an approved issue command to its project's orchestrator." Its User Story ends "so that a new session can be started for an eligible issue". All five acceptance criteria are about routing a request. Under Related, #331 is "sibling watcher and routing service" (gh issue view, 2026-11-14). The watcher design picks "D1 option A (workspace-side poller, pull)" with "No listener, inbound port or address registry" (`issue-watcher.md:39`, `:43`). It also says outright: "Option A makes IronbellyOrg/Coder#477 unnecessary unless B or C is revisited" (`issue-watcher.md:49`). "Superseded" is the design's own word-for-word conclusion, not my reading.
2. **The program's own recommended answers meet the review's close condition.** The review says to close "if the observer stays single-host and TUIBBS is not near" (ADJ line 283, line 605). The one-line Q4 answer the CO-01 reply recommends is "TUI Panel = `superclaude observe`; single-host; read-only" (`merged-requirements.md:353`, CO-01 criterion 2; `:531`). Q18 recommends no channel to the TUIBBS host "before observer R4 or an owner statement that a TUIBBS screen is planned" (`:545`). The review rates the TUIBBS reading at about 15 percent and quotes the owner's 2026-11-08 record, "Background, not deciding" (ADJ line 306). If the owner accepts those recommendations, the narrow-first recommendation contradicts its own trigger. Closing is the answer that stays consistent with them. (This is my inference from the cited rows. The owner has not answered Q4 or Q18.)
3. **Nothing waits on #477.** Part 2.5's action says "Unblocks: nothing in IronClaude" (ADJ line 285). Part 2.7 says the same (ADJ lines 307-308), and so does CO-03 ("none in IronClaude", `merged-requirements.md:355`). An open issue with no consumer and no scheduled build is a standing invitation for someone to build it by accident. Closing removes it.
4. **Fewest new owner decisions and open items.** Text C needs only the Q4 line. Text A needs Q22 or a kept placeholder, the UNVERIFIED owner-token route resolved, and its naming to match the contract note (ADJ line 606; CO-02, `merged-requirements.md:354`). Text A also has an internal conflict the spec had to flag. Its AC 3 says the read route "writes nothing" (ADJ line 631) and its AC 8 says "Each read writes an audit line" (ADJ line 636). CO-03 criterion 5 requires that "the no-write criterion is reconciled with the read-audit requirement" (`merged-requirements.md:355`). That makes four open items in A against one in C.
5. **The reopen conditions are concrete and checkable.** Text C names (1) any of the nine revisit conditions in the watcher design section 2.3 (they exist and number nine: `issue-watcher.md:60`), or (2) observer release R4 or a TUIBBS screen with a task package that needs to read another workspace (ADJ line 661). Each can be observed. That is exactly the boundary diff-analysis A-003 raises. Close does not assume the boundary stays where it is; it states when to reopen.
6. **No send route and no unbuilt design.** Like V1, closing leaves no route that could start a permission-free session (checklist item 9, ADJ line 673). Unlike V1, it commits the Coder repo to no design whose readers (R4, a TUIBBS screen) do not exist. V1 admits its design "may sit unbuilt" (variant-1, "What it costs or risks").

## Weaknesses identified in other variants

1. **V1: text A replaces every section of the issue, so the result is really a new issue under an old number.** Text A has a new title, Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, acceptance criteria and Out of Scope, and the old routing goal moves to Out of Scope (ADJ lines 617-645). The owner's 2026-11-13 comment would then hang under a feature it was not written about. Text C already offers the cleaner form: "open a new issue from the narrowed text" (ADJ line 661). (This is my inference about how readers will see the issue history.)
2. **V1: its "one real need" is in the future.** Text A says the gap is that the observer cannot see another workspace, and "remote fleet is its release R4, gated on cross-host ownership joins" (ADJ line 623). Text A's own "Depends on" line names "observer release R4" (ADJ line 648). So the narrowed issue cannot start until the event that is one of C's reopen conditions. V1 and V3 trigger on the same event. The difference is whether an unbuildable issue sits open in the meantime.
3. **V1: it is not paste-ready, and has one more open item than its variant lists.** V1 lists three open items. The AC 3 / AC 8 write conflict (Strength 4) is a fourth, and the spec makes it a CO-03 gate (`merged-requirements.md:355`).
4. **V1: residual risk it names itself.** "A later Coder change could add a send route without the checklist" (variant-1). An open, unbuilt issue in the Coder repo is where that later change would start. A closed issue has no such opening.
5. **V2: it contradicts a built, owner-chosen design, and no revisit condition can have occurred yet.** D1 option A is an owner decision (`issue-watcher.md:39`). The watcher is not live: "No cron entry is enabled and `enabled` stays `false` until an owner-run live test (FU-1)" (`issue-watcher.md:33`). The nine revisit conditions (`:60`) all describe how the deployed poller behaves (stopped workspaces, latency, duplicate claims, and so on). Without a deployment there is no evidence any of them holds. So V2's step 1 ("state ... why") has no evidence to cite today.
6. **V2: it is blocked on something that does not exist, and it has the biggest attack surface.** Text B is "not paste-ready until the grant format of #339 exists" (ADJ line 606). Every accepted `claim` starts a session with permission checks off (ADJ lines 654, 673). The review calls keep "the least defensible" (ADJ line 605). V2 admits all three ("What it costs or risks"). Its "orchestrator of orchestrators" benefit rests on #339 v2, which is unbuilt (ADJ line 294).
7. **V2: the listener cannot meet its own goal for stopped workspaces.** "a listener runs inside the workspace and cannot start a stopped one" (ADJ line 284). The first watcher revisit condition is "stopped workspaces cause excessive waiting" (`issue-watcher.md:60`). A listener does not fix that, so V2's main justification does not cover the very condition that would reopen it.

## Concessions

1. **The owner's intent.** The owner wrote "Ensure this aligns with Orchestrator and TUI Panel" one day before this question (gh issue view). That asks for alignment, not closure. Closing may go against the owner's most recent signal. V1 answers it better (diff C-005).
2. **The review ranks narrow above close.** The review rates narrow at about 65 percent and close as the conditional alternative (ADJ lines 283, 605). My Strength 2 depends on the owner accepting the recommended Q4 and Q18 answers, and the owner has not answered either.
3. **Text C is not fully paste-ready as written.** It contains a placeholder, "[link to the comment holding option A]" (ADJ line 661). The review calls C paste-ready (ADJ line 606), but that link points to a comment holding text A, which would have to be posted somewhere first, or the clause reworded. My variant's step 2 ("kept in the ledger (or in the closing comment's link)") does not resolve this. The fix: reword the clause to "from the narrowed text recorded in the IronClaude program ledger", or post text A as a closing comment marked "not adopted". CO-01's checks (`merged-requirements.md:353`) cover D-numbers and text A's placeholders, not this link.
4. **Discoverability.** Once closed, the gap "an observer cannot see another workspace" is visible only in a closed issue and the ledger (variant-3, costs). V1 keeps it visible (diff C-006).
5. **Reopen cost.** Reopening means a second round of edits and notifications, and a posted notification cannot be undone (question.md, XC-02 (4)).

## Shared assumption responses

- **A-001 (the watcher was #477's main purpose; "monitor registration" has no separate purpose): ACCEPT.** The issue body supports it directly. Registration exists so "the monitor" can "route prompts securely to the correct workspace" (Feature Summary). The Problem Statement and User Story name only the watcher and starting a session. Related lists #331 as "sibling watcher and routing service". No acceptance criterion describes registration for any other reader (gh issue view, 2026-11-14). One caveat: the "monitor" in the issue is the watcher, not `superclaude observe`. Text A's observer purpose is a new purpose added by the review, not one the issue held.
- **A-002 (cross-repository posting through the single issue editor is accepted): QUALIFY.** The only comment is by `ironbelly` with author association MEMBER (gh issue view). Every post is gated by `GRANT(CO-01,issue-write)` and an owner answer (question.md; XC-02 (4)). So the owner does authorize it, but only per post. Two points stay UNVERIFIED. First, whether "issue-write" covers changing the issue's state (closing), not only commenting or editing. Variant 3 needs a close, so its grant row should name it. Second, whether other Coder-repo readers have a say. The safe default if unanswered is no post at all (`merged-requirements.md:530`).
- **A-003 (single-host and no TUIBBS screen soon holds for the life of the decision): QUALIFY.** It holds today on the program's recommended answers (`merged-requirements.md:531`, `:545`; ADJ line 306). Variant 3 does not need it to hold forever: its reopen condition (2) is that boundary moving (ADJ line 661). If it moves, the cost under V3 is a reopen; under V1 the issue is already open. So A-003 decides which variant is cheaper over time, not which one is safe. Both are safe.
