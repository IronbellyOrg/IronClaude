## The paragraph

The direction is a dense, dark, colour-coded terminal dashboard in the tradition of btop and k9s: a deep navy background with monospaced text, built from titled panels with box-drawing frames (heavy amber frames for anything that needs a person, lighter cyan and grey frames for everything else, panel titles cut into the top border), arranged as side-by-side panes at 160, 116 and 80 columns. Colour carries meaning and nothing else: cyan for running and for headings, green for settled, coral red for failed, amber for human attention and stale data, lavender for frozen or policy-held, grey for unknown, so a person can read the state of eighty sessions at a glance and the one amber panel at the top says what they must decide. Each screen mixes a few reusable parts: a header line with a diamond glyph, the screen name and a status tag on the right; a stacked left pane for the question and the data-freshness window; a main register, table, timeline lane, quilt grid or tree in the centre; a right pane of block-shaded gauges (filled and dotted-shade bars) and large block-digit counters for the headline number; arrow "cables" between boxes for dependencies and handoffs; and a fixed footer key bar (j/k select, tab to change pane, / to filter, enter for evidence, p to hold the view). It should feel like a control room: heavy frames and ANSI colour for the cool factor, but every cell earns its place, with plain-English labels, explicit "partial", "stale" and "unknown" states, and honest footers such as "result is not admission" rather than decoration. The images show panes and panels clearly; they do not show a tab bar, so tabs are an addition this paragraph reads from the owner's request, not from the pictures.
