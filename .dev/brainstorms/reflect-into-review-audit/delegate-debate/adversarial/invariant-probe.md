### Disposition
Per-target "keep both bespoke" is robust (survives probe). The framework-policy rubric is **necessary but not sufficient**: it solves the local decision and gets the targets right, but cannot, alone, control the aggregate property (monoculture) or supply the independent cross-check of reflect itself (A-003). The merged verdict resolves the 4 HIGH items by augmenting the rubric with framework-level invariants (aggregate verifier-heterogeneity guard + a real reflect cross-check + enforceable freeze + owner + staleness re-trigger).
