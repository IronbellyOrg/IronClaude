### Single biggest concern

**INV-001 is the root fault — the consensus has not actually agreed on ONE counter.**
Consensus #1 reads as a single definition but is two: B increments BEFORE diagnosis
on the inbound review ("reviews observed since arm"); C increments AFTER the
fix→push→re-review cycle ("completed cycles"). They coincide on push_count under the
happy path (INV-003) — which is why the debate believes it converged — but diverge on
every non-happy boundary (INV-014 vanished review, INV-013 mixed delta, INV-009/010
re-report identity). The merge must pick ONE timing, write it as the single normative
sentence, and re-derive every fence-post and dedup rule from it; otherwise the
"converged" loop-guard ships with two contradictory mental models and the off-by-one
the whole feature exists to prevent re-enters through the seam between them.
