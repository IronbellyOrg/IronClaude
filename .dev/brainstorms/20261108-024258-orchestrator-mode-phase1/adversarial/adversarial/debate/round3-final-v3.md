# Round 3 Final: Variant 3 (haiku, backend)

- Variant defended: V3, backend persona: API contracts, data integrity, idempotency and operations.
- Round purpose: resolve INV-001, INV-002, INV-003 and INV-005, then close the four open diff points.
- Evidence is documentary only. Orca and git behaviour not shown in the inputs remains UNVERIFIED.

Before taking positions, the strongest forms of the positions I amend are these:

- My Round 2 outage exception gives a person a useful local report when the runtime is down, without claiming that a failed lookup proves no binding. Its weakness is the unchanged seed acceptance criterion, not the usefulness of that report.
- V1's single unavailable class is simple and stops further runtime contact after any ambiguous failure. Its "scanned" join guard already prevents an unreadable lookup from proving person-started origin. These are sound conservative starting points.
- V2's four-class result preserves independently obtainable rows after a local failure. Its uniform diagnostic is easy to implement, and a JSON parse check avoids treating malformed text as evidence. Those strengths do not cover a zero-exit invalid body or a valid JSON value with the wrong schema.
- V2's environment proposal is a small, testable assignment that could suppress optional locks if the relevant git process inherits the environment, while keeping the risk warning. V2's additional status flag similarly aims to remove a known coverage hole rather than just disclose it. Neither proposed runtime effect is verified in the debate inputs.

## Final Position

1. Keep V1's structural base, V2's evidence and tests, and V3's publication safeguards.
2. INV-001, INV-002 and INV-003 have concrete requirement resolutions, type (a).
3. INV-005 remains type (b), an accepted risk needing owner sign-off, not proof of read-only Orca behaviour.
4. Withdraw my Round 2 outage exception: no established Run and no `--run` means exit 2, even during an outage.
5. Accept V2's `call_failed` distinction as a collection policy, with precise failure reasons, not a claim that Orca is healthy.
6. Accept V2's complete-list requirement: an incomplete negative join cannot prove person-started origin.
7. Do not require V2's Orca environment mitigation while its effect remains UNVERIFIED.
8. Match both advocates on C-014, C-017, C-022 and C-034; approval and runtime-evidence gates remain separate.

## Remaining Disagreements

1. **With V1 on INV-002:** take V2's bounded continuation after `call_failed`, rather than treating every later row failure as global unavailability. The earlier steelman of V1 stands: stopping is simpler. My reason is narrower: the failed read supplies no evidence about other rows, so mark its dependants unknown and keep independently obtainable evidence. A later timeout, launch failure or explicit runtime-unavailable signal still stops Orca reads. No claim is made about which real Orca errors are row-local.
2. **With V2 on INV-002 diagnostics:** a zero-exit invalid body must not be presented only as an exit-code failure. The exact reason and captured-schema checks in my requirement make this distinction testable without inventing a runtime response shape. This is an API-contract amendment, not rejection of `call_failed`.
3. **With V1 on INV-003:** "scanned" alone is insufficient. Adopt V2's "complete" condition so the uncaptured next page cannot turn a worker into a person-started session. With V2 there is no substantive disagreement on this guard.
4. **With V2 on INV-005:** do not require the additional `_orca` environment setting or its acceptance test. The files establish only that the setting could matter if the relevant process inherits it, not that Orca does. I do not claim it is harmful or ineffective. Neither the environment assignment nor a mock verifying it establishes the safety property. Keep the explicit risk and owner sign-off unchanged.
5. **With V2 on INV-008:** keep the existing exact status template and disclosed limitation rather than adding a flag whose effect this debate did not verify. This remains a MEDIUM warning, not proof of complete untracked-package discovery.

No final-position disagreement remains on C-014, C-017, C-022 or C-034. V1's list of earlier V2/V3 disagreements must be read with the later V2 file and this file, not treated as current positions. The owner still decides INV-005 risk acceptance and WC-14 wording. Exact Orca response and continuation shapes remain UNVERIFIED. Advocate agreement is not owner sign-off or runtime evidence.

## Final Concessions

1. Withdraw my R2 support for exit 0 without a Run during an outage. A missing established binding is not permission to bypass the seed's exit-2 requirement. Accept V2's explicit binding-established-then-lost case.
2. Accept V2's `call_failed` class with source-specific unknown evidence and bounded continuation. Amend its diagnostics and response-shape validation, not its basic distinction.
3. Accept V2's complete-list negative-join condition and the tri-state origin field. My original unjoined-transcript wording was unsafe with missing or truncated Orca data.
4. Expand my C-014 and C-017 positions to the three-way merge; make C-034's new-files-first sequencing explicit. Retain V3's C-022 wording without adding a speculative source dependency.
5. Retain the publication and idempotency limits: new exclusive output per invocation, no overwrite, no success JSON before completed publication, and no claim of atomic publication across an uncatchable termination.
6. INV-005 is acknowledged, not proved fixed: owner sign-off remains required. No live release check or speculative runtime mitigation becomes a hidden Phase 1 acceptance criterion.
