## 4 Implications, benefits, risks, costs

Benefits: one false "creation rule" and one unapproved "duty grant" statement are gone before anyone builds; 516 becomes small and testable; the owner's stated end state (nothing manual) survives through the S17 flip.
Risks: (a) the owner may read "withdrawn" as a reversal of a 2026-11-18 decision; the comment says it is a creation rule fixed, end state kept.
