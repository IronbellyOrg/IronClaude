## Required resolution for each HIGH UNADDRESSED finding

**INV-001.** Remove "worst 150.0 s" for F3, F4 and F7, and "worst 330 s" for F6, as cap guarantees. For each mid-run design the spec states three numbers:

- the planned end, B + W;
- the finish-signal bound, PANEL_FINISH_AT + turn_p90 + T_report_p90;
- the enforced ceiling, the hang guard at 1.5 x B_A1, which counts as `SLO_BREACH`.

It adopts V5's rule `PANEL_FINISH_AT = T0 + min(1.20 B_f, 1.35 B_f - turn_p90 - T_report_p90)`. Spike 4 measures the last-turn duration for luna and sonnet. REQ-S3 measures the act rate on "finish now". The spec says plainly that for slots 2-5 the +35% cap is audited by the SLO budget, not enforced. It also requires B + W <= PANEL_FINISH_AT, so that the signal never fires on the planned path (see INV-008).

**INV-002.** One wall-clock deadline covers every process that can write a phase's lines. The phase-2 deadline `T0 + 1.30 B_A1` covers the resume or fresh fallback, the challenges and any H2 re-ask. If a re-ask cannot start with at least one start plus one turn before the deadline, it is skipped: the invalid phase-2 lines are dropped and phase 1 stands. In A1 and the mid-run arms, the H2 re-ask is logged as `reask_s`, and the cap check uses measured elapsed time that includes it. The words "compliance-free hard bound" may appear only with this rule in place.

**INV-003.** The challenge count becomes `n = min(cap, floor((box - start_cost - margin) / (2 x turn_p90)))`, recomputed after the resume attempt: start_cost is 6 s on a resume and about 16 s on the fresh fallback. Phase 2 is told to emit one complete findings line after each challenge. On a timeout, the combiner keeps every complete, parser-valid P2 line from the partial output instead of discarding all of them. The spec states that at planning B luna B gets n = 1 whenever turn_p90 > 7.2 s.

**INV-004.** The CONVERGENCE Jaccard is computed on `origin:"own"` lines only: phase-1 or pre-delivery findings, excluding P2-F and peer-tagged lines. Phase-2 independence is judged by ECHO on real-anchor slots. Also hash-split ember's qualifying anchors disjointly between the two lunas (as F4 and F7 already do), so that the design does not create luna-luna overlap. V2's "alarm in at most 2 of 12 runs" guard applies to this own-line Jaccard.

**INV-005.** Strike the claim that A5 vs A4 isolates "challenge vs shared findings". The spec states that A5 vs A4 compares two whole designs that differ in sender session, content source, move type and target issues. To answer SEED:47, add a within-arm contrast to F7. Each ember card is rendered at random, seeded per card and logged per slot, in one of two forms:

- challenge form: anchor plus a required finite check;
- finding form: anchor plus ember's own claim.

Report conversion per form. Otherwise say SEED:47 is answered descriptively only.

**INV-006.** State the distinctness criterion once and apply it to all five slots. To keep F6, define its coverage source as the findings blocks of peers that have finished, (Task, category) at exit ("where peers concluded"), rendered as requests for uncovered Tasks to a running strong model. This contrasts with F3's live tool-call telemetry ("where peers looked"), and V4-5's original watcher already does it. If the slot cannot be kept distinct, replace it with the remaining unused family F2 (mid-run method relay, the only I6 trigger).

**INV-007.** The classifier section lists, for each catalogue move, its observable tool-call signature. Moves with no signature (M3, M5, M6, M8, M9, M10) are marked unobservable and may not be described as "not done by any panel member". Luna's injections are labelled a timed static prompt, booked to the prompting share (A2 comparator). F3's collaboration content is restated as ember's search-move gaps plus the "source.md never opened" check. F6 draws reading-move requests only from findings-derived coverage (INV-006), never from telemetry.

**INV-008.** F3, F4 and F7 get a per-package count formula like F1's: `k = min(2, floor((0.35 B_A1(pkg) - hooks - turn_p90 - T_report_p90) / (2 x turn_p90)))` facts, cards or injections per luna. At the planning values (B = 116, turn 6.4, T_report 6.4) this gives 2. At B = 90 it gives 1. Spike 4 publishes per-package B_A1 and k before any arm runs, and every "+x%" figure in the spec is labelled as valid only at B = 116.
