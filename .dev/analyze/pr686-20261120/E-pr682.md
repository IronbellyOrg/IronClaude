## Case for and against

For the deletion:
- Never ran live; the handoff had one throwaway-issue test (#452).
- Known weakness: an agent on the same GitHub login could apply the approval label (Decision 4, accepted risk L-1).
- It needed a coordinator, a grant row per issue and two sessions per approval; the owner accepted the latency only "for now" (Q36).
- Clay ruled it out; the design replaces poller, coordinator and gate as one system, and keeping it would force a shim for symbols PR A removes.
- Careful scope: guard rule 2 kept, independent guard review passed, suite and lint passed.

Against the deletion:
- It reverses ironbelly's merged work (#584, #591, three days old) and the answers to Decisions 2, 3, 6 and Q35 to Q38 without naming them or recording agreement from the person who gave them. #682 merged about an hour after opening with no human review.
- "Reviewed at creation" is policy; the code checks only that headings are filled. No person can say "build it" or "no" between plan and implementation.
- "Assignment is safe" is marked UNVERIFIED in the issue; design OQ6 finds two gaps.
- Stale claim docs, watcher runbook and installed copies describe a handoff that no longer exists, and ironbelly's worktree depends on the deleted code.
- PR B, which would hold the replacement double-start protection, is not built. Mitigation: the watcher is disabled.

Caveats: the owner-answers path in the brief was missing, so the tracked master copy was used; the roles of ironbelly and clayphi relative to "the owner" are UNVERIFIED.
