## Method and limits
- `SPEC:N` = line N of `.dev/orca-command-refactor/specs/orca-worker-contract-spec.md` AFTER the revision unless marked "(before)", which means the line number in the Task 4 version (identical to `.dev/orca-command-refactor/specs/history/spec-v1.md`). `REV:N` = line N of `.dev/orca-command-refactor/review/spec-panel.md`. `INV-A:N`, `INV-B:N`, `CAPS:N` = line N of `inventory/commands-a.md`, `commands-b.md`, `orca-capabilities.md`. `README:N` = `.dev/orca-command-refactor/README.md`.
- Each of the 22 findings (`REV:264-287`) was checked against the spec text and the three inventories, which are the only evidence in the sandbox. A citation that points outside the workspace (`src/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `~/.claude`, `.dev/evals/...`, `.dev/codegraph-migration/...`) is UNVERIFIED here unless an inventory quotes it; the reviewer's own uses of `command.md` (`SPCMD`) are treated as evidence about a command's text, not about the repo baseline.
- Verdicts: **applied** (the finding is confirmed and the spec changed), **applied in part** (confirmed in substance; one sub-claim or option rejected, with the reason), **rejected** (not confirmed). No finding is rejected outright; two have a rejected sub-claim.
- Spec size: 57763 bytes before, 80732 bytes after (+22969). The growth is the new rules, examples (section 2.4), checks (AC-0.5, AC-2.6 to AC-2.8, section 7.4) and the revision log.
- Placeholder check: `grep -niE 'TBD|TODO|FIXME|XXX|placeholder|lorem'` on the revised spec returns nothing (the earlier match was the tool name `TodoWrite` at `SPEC:158`). The `<...>` names in commands and the `ORCA` notation at `SPEC:217` are templates the spec defines, not unfinished text.
