THEME "diffgate": mechanical delta scope. After the first review, a re-review must not be a fresh whole-document review. Design a mechanism where the re-review surface is COMPUTED (a unified diff of the saved baseline against the current document, plus the list of prior blocking findings with their ids), the reviewer is handed only that surface plus read-only context, and every new finding must cite a changed line or a prior finding id. A finding that cites neither is recorded as `deferred: pre-existing` and can never be blocking after review 1. Work out: who computes the diff (a script under src/superclaude/, or a fixed command sequence), how the loop proves the reviewer stayed inside the surface (a checker that rejects a report whose findings do not anchor to the diff), what happens to genuine fix-induced defects on changed lines (they are in scope, so the design must stop them from regenerating forever: for example, a changed line may be re-reviewed once, and an unchanged-since-last-review line is never re-opened), and what happens to a real defect the first review missed (deferred list, surfaced once at the end, not looped on). Compare against 427's `--re-review --baseline` flags and say what they leave to prose that you make mechanical.
