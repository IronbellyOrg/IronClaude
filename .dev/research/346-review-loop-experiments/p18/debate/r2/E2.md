## 2. Defence of E2

**"Stage 0 pass is overstated."** True. Stage 0 made zero Jev calls, and its status is BLOCKED because candidate-pool misses were 11, over the limit of 2 (`p15/e2/results.json`). The a-priori prefilter fix did not clear it (pass 2 still shows 5/16 pool coverage). "Stage 0 passed" in the matrix is loose. What stands is headroom: H=3 of 16, static top-12 covers 2/16. Only one H case is a real defect (s0-22, D16); two are seeded chains.

**"Wrong lever."** I concede this more fully than in round 1. `p17/score/defect_support.json`: D09 is raised by 7 arms and D10 by 6, yet both are fixed in 0 of 6 specs in 7 of 8 arms (`perdefect.md`). D06-D08 and D11 are raised in 6-7 arms. Their bottleneck is the reviser, not the first review. My round-1 prediction that D07-D12 raise counts would rise was wrong; they are already raised almost everywhere. Only D25, D26 and D28 among blocking defects have 3 or fewer supporting arms. So E2's expected effect on resid_blk is about 0, and invisible at n=6.

**Residual case.** The unfound tail: D14, D16, D17, D19 and D37-D40 each come from the ab4 arm alone, and 14 of 44 defects came from one arm family. Mostly medium defects (weight 2 in the weighted score).

**"The panel makes it redundant."** Partly true and untested: the panel adds readers, not directed reading.

**Cost.** About 4.8k input tokens (1.4% of the panel). It fails open to document-order hints, adds no slot and has no false-closure path.
