# E3 opening case (advocate): confirmation-only finding screen

Paths are under SP (the scratchpad dir).

## 1. Mechanism
Plugs in between ADJ and the clean VF. Harness code builds a packet per merged blocking finding (severity and reasoning stripped, claim plus up to 2 verbatim quotes checked in the spec, `p15/e3/packets.py`). Jev answers two Noul questions plus a null-case companion (`outcome_follows`, `texts_conflict`, `evidence_sufficient`, `p15/e3/questions.py`). All at or above 0.7 means CONFIRMED and the finding skips VF. Everything else (low, missing, malformed, quote not found) goes to VF unchanged. CRITICAL is code-retained and never sent to Jev. Jev cannot reject, so blocker loss is structurally 0 (`p15/e3/results.json`). E3 changes only who verifies, not what is fixed.

## 2. Evidence it addresses a real failure
Modest, and I say so. It does not touch the main failure (reviewers raise defects, the reviser leaves them: p17/LEDGER-RESULTS.md, baseline 17.3 of 20 blocking left). What it touches is the VF step. In ab4, VF1 cost 4,755 / 6,737 / 4,590 / 9,187 / 6,441 / 6,191 output tokens (`p16/ab4/runs/d4-r*/state.json`), mean 6.3k on 6 to 9 merged findings, about 0.9k per finding. Ledger defects that VF could wrongly reject are the multi-source ones (D06, D07, D11, D12: raised by most reviewers). Those are the ones Jev tends to confirm (`texts_conflict` at 0.7 or above was 6 of 6 blocking, `p15/e3/offline-results.md`).

## 3. Complementarity
Weak. The panel already gives a stronger confirmation signal than Jev: a finding raised independently by 2 or more tiers. ADJ already adjudicates. So E3 duplicates ADJ and panel agreement on exactly the findings it would confirm. It does not interact with lint or the AC sweep. Paired with E1/E4 it is harmless but acts at a different stage (pre-revise, not fix closure), so it reinforces nothing.

## 4. Cost
Savings ceiling: if automation is 0.18 (offline) and VF cost is linear in findings, 0.18 x ~10 merged findings x 0.9k = ~1.6k tokens saved. ab5 loop is about 410k, so ~0.4%. Even 100% automation saves about 9k, 2%. Jev: about 1.3k input tokens per call (126,333 / 96 calls); 10 findings x 2 to 3 calls = ~35k tokens = $0.0015 per run. Offline total was $0.0053. Added harness complexity: packet builder, policy, VF prompt filtering.

## 5. Strongest case against, and does it sink it?
Yes, I concede it largely does.
- The offline gate returned NO-GO-OFFLINE: automation 0.179 (10/56) is under the frozen 0.20 minimum (`p15/e3/results.json`). A near miss, but thresholds were frozen before the data.
- Quality of confirmations is poor: Jev false-confirmation rate was 0.30 (3 of 10), against 0.20 for the conventional verifier. The `outcome_follows` bin at 0.7 or above was only 54% blocking (13 of 24), so it is badly calibrated. At 0.6 the error rate was 43%.
- Applicability to ab5: the gate used single-reviewer findings and a per-finding packet. ab5 findings come from a 4-model panel after merge, so the population is more often true and automation could clear 0.20, but that is unmeasured. A false confirmation just reproduces today's no-verifier behavior (baseline sends every blocker to the reviser), so harm is bounded. But bounded harm with a ~0.4% savings is a poor reason to spend one of three slots.
- Measurability: the effect on residual blocking should be zero. With sd about 2 and n=6, E3 is undetectable and untestable by the ledger, except as a no-regression check.

## 6. Falsifiable prediction
If I am right (E3 as a pure cost trim): residual blocking within +/-2 of ab5 (indistinguishable), fixed of 44 within +/-3 of ab5, introduced-defect hits unchanged (0.3 to 1.3), and VF output tokens down 10 to 25% (about 0.6 to 1.6k per run), total loop tokens down under 1%. Falsified if the 6 runs show automation below 10%, any ledger blocker defect that was in the merged list and VF-rejected in ab5 but accepted via Jev and then wrongly edited (WORSE or MOOT_BAD rises), or loop tokens that do not fall.

Bottom line: E3 is safe but near-valueless on ab5. I would pick it for the top 3 only if the others are excluded by their own gates.
