# Round 2.5 — Invariant Probe (Independent Fault-Finder)

**Role:** Neutral fault-finder, not advocating either variant.
**Target:** The emerging consensus from R1+R2 (12-week timeline; audit-in-M1; lockout-in-M1; in-process async email for v1.0 with Bull deferred; V1 state machines; V2 buffer + post-GA + staffing).
**Method:** Probe load-bearing assumptions for state, guard, count, collection, interaction, and sufficiency failures using the merged PRD+TDD as ground truth.

## Summary

**Total findings:** 26
**By status:**

- ADDRESSED: 3 (INV-004, INV-009, INV-012, INV-018) = 4
- UNADDRESSED: 22

**By severity:**

- HIGH: 8 (INV-001, INV-005, INV-006, INV-013, INV-017, INV-021, INV-022, INV-023, INV-026) = 9
- MEDIUM: 11 (INV-002, INV-003, INV-007, INV-008, INV-010, INV-015, INV-019, INV-020, INV-024, INV-025) = 10
- LOW: 7 (INV-004, INV-009, INV-011, INV-012, INV-014, INV-016, INV-018) = 7

**Robustness verdict:** The emerging consensus is structurally sound on roadmap-level decisions (timeline, milestone placement, state machine adoption) but has **critical gaps in interaction effects and sufficiency**: in particular, the in-process SendGrid retry contradicts the 200ms p95 budget (INV-021), the audit-log nullability and timing-symmetry interaction with enumeration-resistance is unanalyzed (INV-005, INV-006, INV-008), the bcrypt-cost-12 latency budget violation remains unresolved by consensus (INV-026), and SOC2 audit-log immutability + GDPR right-to-erasure are necessary-not-sufficient conditions the merged roadmap does not yet satisfy (INV-022, INV-025). These nine HIGH-severity items block convergence per AD-1 until either addressed in the merged spec or explicitly accepted as residual risk with named decision owners.
