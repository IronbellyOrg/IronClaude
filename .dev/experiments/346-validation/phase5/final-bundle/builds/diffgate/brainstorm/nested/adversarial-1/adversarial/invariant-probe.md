Resolution (INV-001): A structural stop leaves the ledger in `reviewing` with an attempt counter; `open` from `reviewing` is allowed once for the same review number, reusing the same snapshot and surface; a second structural stop sets a terminal `invalid` state that every later call refuses, escalates to the user, and is recorded as a failed attempt that is never replaced.

Resolution (INV-004): Add `DG-STATUS <id> withdrawn quote=... reason=...`, legal only for an id the reviser declined in the preceding fix map; a withdrawn id leaves the open blocking set but stays in the R3 proxy at its recorded label, and any withdrawal turns the verdict into a scoped PASS that cannot earn clean-PASS credit.

Resolution (INV-015): Define the R3 proxy as one function applied to every arm's review-3 report and ledger: CRITICAL or MAJOR labels on (a) findings first raised in review 3, deferred and demoted included, and (b) prior blocking findings marked open or regressed in review 3, at their recorded label, each id counted once within review 3 only; recompute the control and Arm B with the same function before comparing.

Resolution (INV-016): Compute the F1 share over all-discovered review-3 critical+major findings (blocking, deferred and demoted), each classified by ledger ancestry as on a blocking-fix hunk, on a minor or unmapped edit, or on unchanged text, and state in the pre-registration that the blocking-only share is 100% by construction.

Resolution (INV-027): The last non-blank line of every report is the verdict line; the deferred appendix and any scoped-acceptance note are written above the Reviser brief and the verdict, and a test asserts the last line is exactly `verdict: PASS` or `verdict: FAIL`.

Resolution (INV-034): Pre-register the S1 claim as the combined treatment (script plus a published brief diff) and state that a pass does not support "mechanical over prose" until a briefs-only arm with the same brief lines and no script is run.

Resolution (INV-036): Pre-register before any replicate that S1 at n=2 returns only one of three result classes (bar met; bar missed with F1; bar missed without F1), that the spread check is reported but cannot be met unless the treatment mean is below 2.0, and that any claim of a proven effect needs a replicate count fixed in advance.
