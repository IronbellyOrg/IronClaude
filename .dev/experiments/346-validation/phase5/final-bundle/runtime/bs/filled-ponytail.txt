WHY THIS BRAINSTORM EXISTS (measured, not assumed)
A controlled experiment re-ran the recorded incident (a spec reviewed by `/sc:spec-panel`, fixed, re-reviewed, three reviews, two fix cycles) in three arms, two runs each: pre-427 text (control), 427 text (treatment), 427 text plus its own `--re-review --baseline` flags (Arm B). Results:
- 427 roughly halves spec growth (control +63.5%, 427 +29.7%, Arm B +30.0% after two fix cycles).
- 427 does NOT make the loop converge. No run of any arm reached a clean PASS. Open critical+major findings in the third review: control 8 and 4, 427 6 and 5, Arm B 6 and 1.
- Root cause that survives 427: 89% (review 2) and 100% (review 3) of the new findings sit on lines the previous fix changed (`origin=changed`). A fresh review of the whole document keeps finding defects that the last fix introduced or exposed, whatever the reviser is told.
- Other observed contributors: severity labels drift upward between rounds, revisers add mechanisms and sections for minor findings, and nothing mechanical stops the loop (the verdict is the reviewer's judgement; the cap of 3 reviews is prose).
