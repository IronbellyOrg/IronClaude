## D4. Treatment
Re-run the scenario with the same starting document, the same briefs (verbatim) and the same review tool and flags as the original, in a worktree where PR 427 is active: base the worktree on master at or after 95f2c6e4, then run `make sync-dev` so the project `.claude/` copy carries the new spec-panel text. ## D5. Metrics (per scenario, per round, for baseline and re-run)
M1 rounds until a clean pass (or the cap/stop). M2 blocking-grade findings per round. M3 share of findings whose cause is the previous round's fix (reviewer origin tag, or the analyst's re-derivation, said which). M4 document size at start and after each round (bytes, lines), and growth percent. M5 tokens or tool calls per round if recoverable.

## D6. What counts as a significant delta (fixed now)
A scenario shows a significant improvement only if ALL hold in BOTH replicate sessions, in the same direction:
(a) last-round blocking findings <= 50 percent of the baseline's last round, or a clean pass is reached in fewer rounds than baseline;
(b) document growth over the loop <= 70 percent of the baseline's growth (or the document shrinks);
(c) the effect is larger than the spread between the two replicates (replicates disagree by more than the claimed effect means "not significant").
Anything else is "not significant" and is discarded as noise.
"427 proven": at least 3 of the 5 scenarios show a significant improvement, or both spec-panel scenarios (if two or more exist) do and none gets worse by the same test. Otherwise 427 is not proven, and the 5 brainstorm teams start.
