## Why some briefs cannot be reused literally (RECONSTRUCTED templates)

The R2/R3 briefs name outcomes of the original run. A replay whose round-1 review finds a different set of findings must substitute them:

| Template (RECONSTRUCTED) | Derived from | Only change from the verbatim text |
|---|---|---|
| `templates/T07-R2-reviewer.TEMPLATE.txt` | T07 | "the 24 round-1 findings" to `{{R1_COUNT}}` |
| `templates/T08-R2-reviser.TEMPLATE.txt` | T08 | "N1-N13, plus the 2 overstated citations" to `{{R2_FIRST_ID}}-{{R2_LAST_ID}}`, `{{R2_CITATION_COUNT}}`; "both citations" to "every overstated citation" |
| `templates/T09-R3-reviewer.TEMPLATE.txt` | T09 | "N1-N13 and both overstated citations" to `{{R2_FIRST_ID}}-{{R2_LAST_ID}}` and "every overstated citation" |
| `templates/T10-R3-reviser.GUARDRAILS-ONLY.RECONSTRUCTED.txt` | T10 | clause (A) and S1-S3 removed (they name mechanisms of the original spec); `{{R3_FIRST_ID}}`, `{{R3_LAST_ID}}`, `{{R3_SPEC_BYTES}}` |

T05 and T06 contain no outcome-specific text and are reused verbatim. If the replay's round-1 review finds exactly 24 findings and round-2 numbers its findings N1-N13 with 2 overstated citations, the verbatim T07-T09 task-text can be used unchanged.
