## Ten deliberately different compositions
| Concept | Dominant visual geometry | Five-second target |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Bridge | Wide owner-row cockpit with narrow intervention rail and footer change ribbon | Who needs me and which mission is affected? |
| Fleet Quilt | Eight stable orchestrator territories with individually labeled session cells; separate detail drawer | Where are clusters of waits, stale observations or failed results? |
| Time Loom | Shared-time-axis lanes and discontinuous observation-gap bands; bottom inspection strip | Was time spent working, reviewing, queueing or waiting? |
| Worktree Atlas | Branch/base tree, ownership column and selected-path overlap inspector | Which work is isolated, shared, unmapped or policy-frozen? |
| Decision Desk | Spacious question cards, owner/affected-scope rail and conversation excerpt | What specific decision is mine now? |
| Proof Gate | Revision-pinned matrix, report/admission rails and missing-evidence gutter | Are outcomes supported by current evidence? |
| Budget Engine | Separate spend and resource instruments with coverage rail, units and unknowns | What is measured, and what can I not account for? |
| Relay Map | Bounded declared handoff nodes with meaningful orthogonal patch cables and inset evidence | Which cross-orchestrator relationship is holding work? |
| Delta Deck | Persistent unresolved strip plus new/resolved/worsened/unchanged journal lanes | What changed since my last successful observation? |
| Watchkeeper | Low-noise large attention summary, short handover narrative and compact evidence strip | Can I step away, and what is still unverified? |
