Task 11 then requires that crash to release the draft.

The other reading omits the sha, the release holds, and the Task 11 check fails. The crash tests also say not to pass a hand-written branch string. They do not say where the pushed sha comes from.

Grading: "An implementer who follows the stated text as written builds a resume function with no pushed sha, and the published-fix crash either stays draft or is marked ready without proving the head." Which of those two happens is a guess. Medium, not blocking. The safe failure is a stuck draft. The unsafe one marks the pull request ready when the head is no longer the fix that was pushed.
