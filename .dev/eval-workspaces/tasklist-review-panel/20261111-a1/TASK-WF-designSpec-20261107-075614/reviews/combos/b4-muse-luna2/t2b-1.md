Fix direction: state the test as the task body does (the resume hook is not gated, tested by the absence of `L3 only` or equivalent gate wording on the resume clause), and drop "when it names an L3 or `draft` condition" from the acceptance criterion, or split the bullet into an ungated resume sentence and a separate L3 `off` sentence and scope the wording test to the first sentence only.
