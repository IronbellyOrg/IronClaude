**F1 (MAJOR, graded Medium, non-blocking). Resume row 4 cannot get the inputs `release_decision` needs. Experts: Nygard, Whittaker.**
- **Attack:** I can break this specification by the **Sequence Attack**. The invariant at **Task 4, `resume_decision` signature and row 4** fails when **a crash happens after a normal `push_completed`, or after a recovered `landed` push, and the run resumes**.
- **Concrete attack:**
  - The stated signature is `resume_decision(*, mode, ordinal, guard, read, pending, dangling_branch, published)`.
  - Row 4 must "re-enter `release_decision` with `push_completed=True`".
  - `release_decision` also needs `applied_edits`, `target_sha` and `cycle_id`. Row 8 compares `read.head_sha == target_sha`, and row 7 releases when `applied_edits == 0`.
  - The signature supplies none of these, and the fold dict has no `target_sha`.
  - If the implementer passes no `target_sha`, row 8 never matches and the PR stays draft after a good push. That is the case Task 11's crash tests expect to release.
  - If the implementer passes `applied_edits=0`, row 7 releases with no publication proof.
- **Grading sentence:** An implementer who follows the stated text as written builds a `resume_decision` that cannot supply `target_sha` or `applied_edits` to `release_decision`, and that is wrong because row 4 then holds forever or releases unproven. The text never says whether to widen the signature or where those values come from. That is a requirement left unstated, so it is Medium.
- **Corroboration:** the finished build in the repo adds `applied_edits`, `target_sha` and `cycle_id` parameters.
- **Recommendation:** state the extra inputs in Task 4 and in the Resume section of Task 8. Name the source of `target_sha` (the held cycle's push record). Add a Task 4 criterion that row 4 returns `RELEASE(pushed)` only when the head equals that `target_sha`. Priority: Medium.
