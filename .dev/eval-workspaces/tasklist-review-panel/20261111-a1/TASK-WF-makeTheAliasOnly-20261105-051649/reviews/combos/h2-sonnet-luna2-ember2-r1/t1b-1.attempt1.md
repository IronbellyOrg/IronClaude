**F1, Medium (MAJOR label, not blocking): the launcher spec exports the three variables for every tier-mode owner.**
- *Sentence:* an implementer who follows Task 9 builds a launcher that exports `opus` to `claude-gw-tier1a` for every tier-mode launch, and that is wrong for an owner limited to other tiers (for example tier2 and tier3) because `model: opus` helpers then ask for a tier the owner may not use.
- *Evidence:*
  - The launcher already applies owner limits: its text says "only for a tier this owner may use" (`ccsession` lines 892 and 1202 in the tree I inspected).
  - The test file has `test_named_tier_checks_definition_and_owner`.
  - `.dev/issues/384/launcher-access-aware.md` records the same gap.
- *Why not blocking:* the document never states the limit, and the source's owner decision lists the exports without it. Neither Task 6's Known consequences nor Task 9 mentions limited owners.
- *Raised by:* Nygard (failure mode), Wiegers (unstated assumption).

**F2, Medium: the scratch copy in Task 9 breaks unless its paths change.**
- *Sentence:* an implementer who copies the harness as stated, with only the log addition, builds a module that cannot import `models`, and that is wrong because the Task 9 criterion requires an AssertionError, not an import error.
- *Evidence:*
  - `tests/unit/test_ccsession_tiers.py` sets `REPO_ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]` and `SKILL_DIR` from it.
  - It runs `import models` at module level and launches `SKILL_DIR / "ccsession"`.
  - Under `./artifacts/t9-scratch/` in the package folder, `parents[2]` is the package folder, not the repo.
- *Why not blocking:* the text does not forbid adjusting the path, and the implementer sees the failure on the first run. The spec just never says to change `REPO_ROOT`.
