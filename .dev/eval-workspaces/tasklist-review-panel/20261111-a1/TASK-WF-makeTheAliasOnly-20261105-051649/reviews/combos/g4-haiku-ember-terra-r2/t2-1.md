## Follow-up candidates

### F4: Unconditional tier exports ignore owner access

**Severity:** MAJOR, non-blocking. The proposed follow-up exports all three tier IDs whenever tier mode is active. The current launcher checks the owner’s usable tiers before setting each variable; `models.py` also restricts tier selection by owner. For an owner limited to lower tiers, the proposed exports can point aliases at unavailable tiers instead of retaining inherited values. The plan does not specify how this case should behave.

**Recommendation:** Consider documenting and testing restricted-owner and missing-tier launches before implementing the follow-up.

The probe does not establish the general rule, so the limitation should be stated as an observation rather than extrapolated.

**Recommendation:** Include the observed exception and its limits in the reference page.
