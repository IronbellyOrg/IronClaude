### F2. The launcher follow-up spec exports all three tier aliases for every tier-mode launch, ignoring per-owner tier access and the `[1m]` alias form (Nygard, Fowler)

Task 9 specifies the three export lines exactly as `ANTHROPIC_DEFAULT_OPUS_MODEL=claude-gw-tier1a`, `..._SONNET_MODEL=claude-gw-tier1b`, `..._HAIKU_MODEL=claude-gw-tier2`, gated only on tier mode being on. The dependency it specifies against does not work that way: `models.py` computes the tiers the workspace owner may use (`tier_access`, `resolve_tiers`: "The tiers the workspace owner may use"), the launcher already in the tree exports each variable only when that tier is on the owner's usable list and in the `[1m]` form (`ccsession` lines 892-898: "Map an alias to its tier only for a tier this owner may use... A tier not on the list... leaves the inherited variable alone"), and `tests/unit/test_ccsession_tiers.py` pins the limited-owner cases (`test_limited_owner_gets_only_the_haiku_alias_variable`, `test_limited_owner_keeps_inherited_alias_values`, lines 3309-3340). The same unconditional wording is what Tasks 3, 6 and 7 would publish in the reference page and user docs.

Grading check: An implementer who follows the stated text as written builds a launcher that sets all three variables to the bare tier ids on any tier-mode launch, and that is wrong because a limited owner (for example one who may use only tier2 and tier3) would have the inherited opus and sonnet values replaced by pointers to tiers they cannot use, and every tier-mode launch would lose the `[1m]` alias form the gateway tier contract uses.

Fix direction: state the per-tier usable-list gate and the `[1m]` alias values in the Task 9 spec, and carry the same qualification into the Task 3, 6 and 7 wording.
