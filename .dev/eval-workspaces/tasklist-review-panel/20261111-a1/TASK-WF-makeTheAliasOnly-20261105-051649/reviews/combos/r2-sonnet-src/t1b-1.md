- **Impact.** An owner limited to Tiers 2-4 would get `opus` mapped to `claude-gw-tier1a`, a tier the owner may not use. Helper agents with `model: opus` would then fail.

- **Grade.** Medium, not blocking. It is a requirement the document never states. The stated Task 9 acceptance criteria still pass.
- **Related gap.** Task 6's "Known consequences" list also omits limited owners.
