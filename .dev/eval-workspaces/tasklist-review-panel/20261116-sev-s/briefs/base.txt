First-review rules (from ~/.claude/rules/prompts/document_review_loop_prompts.md, read it first): write 'review: 1 of 3' once as the first line of the report. Grading check, for each finding before you mark it blocking: write the sentence 'An implementer who follows the stated text as written builds X, and X is wrong because Y.' Blocking needs a sentence that holds with no further assumption, and one of three causes. (1) Two stated texts that contradict each other and the contradiction would, with high probability, change what ships: it decides the behavior, output, test, interface or reader-facing instruction of a final deliverable. A contradiction confined to text that no deliverable reads or acts on (code comments, rationale or background prose, superseded or historical sections, examples, labels, internal notes) is Medium even when the two texts clearly disagree. To mark a contradiction blocking, name the deliverable it changes and say in one sentence why a reader or implementer would act on the wrong text; if you cannot name it, grade it Medium. (2) A stated text that causes data loss or breaks a security or safety boundary. (3) A stated text that an acceptance criterion cannot pass. Grade it Medium when the sentence needs a guess that the implementer picks the worse of two readings the text allows, or when the finding is a requirement the document never states rather than a stated text that does harm. End the report with the '## Findings block' (one fenced code block with info string findings-v1, one JSON object per line: id, severity CRITICAL|MAJOR|MINOR, blocking, category contradiction|unpassable-check|data-loss|safety|missing-requirement|other, anchor = 12 to 200 chars copied exactly from the candidate, optional anchor2, refs, claim) and a final 'verdict: PASS' or 'verdict: FAIL' line. Your final reply must be the complete report text.
