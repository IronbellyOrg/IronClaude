# Collaboration designs: counted results, 2026-11-14

Five designs that let reviewers on a panel exchange messages were each run on three panels (ember = muse-ember-1.0 + 2x gpt-6-luna, haiku = claude-haiku-5-5 + 2x luna, sonnet = claude-sonnet-5-5 + 2x luna) against an isolated baseline in the same harness, three packages per row, judged blind by Opus. The time-cap exception was on for every collaboration row (owner order: the +35% rule may be exceeded).

## Conclusion

No design shows a reliable gain. Design 4 looked best after 6 runs and did not hold up at 12. Designs 1 to 3 and 5 showed no gain or a loss. Recommendation: stop the collaboration line.
