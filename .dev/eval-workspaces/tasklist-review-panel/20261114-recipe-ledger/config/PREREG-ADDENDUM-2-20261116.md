# Pre-registration addendum 2 (2026-11-16, before the first hold-out run; after the ground truth was written, before any run)

Context: see `PREREG-ADDENDUM-20261116.md`. This addendum records what changed from its item 1 and why.

1. **Documents actually used: HO-2, HO-3, HX-1, HX-3** (4 plans), not HO-1 to HO-4. HO-1 and HO-4 were dropped by the independent ground-truth author: the plan files in the repository are later versions than the ones their earlier review reports examined (the reviewed versions were never committed), so all documented defects were already fixed in the file under test. HX-2 was dropped: its base commit lacks five files the plan edits (they existed only uncommitted when the plan was written). HX-1, HX-2, HX-3 are plans found afterwards whose repository version matches the reviewed one by hash; they are not study packages. They appeared as "other material" in the generality investigation and were not used to design the recipes.
2. **Ground truth:** HO-2 10 items (4 blocking), HO-3 12 (6), HX-1 2 (0), HX-3 12 (0): 36 items, 10 blocking. Written by a fresh Sonnet session (owner's choice) that was forbidden to read recipes, cards, derivation results or study ground truth; every quote was checked to appear in the plan file; `class` is `unassigned` (the author did not know the classes), so no per-class reach is reported for this set. The files are committed and their sha256 are in `config/prereg.json` `gt_sha256` before any run.
3. **Runs:** 4 documents x 3 arms (Z0, K, RL) x 5 replicates = 60 panel runs, as in addendum 1. Low power is expected: HX-1 has only 2 items and two documents have no blocking item, so the blocking-item comparison cannot be decided on this set and will be reported as descriptive only.
4. **Launcher change after the freeze (not a frozen file):** `bin/run-schedule.sh` rebuilds a sandbox with git-ignored leftovers before a run; `bin/run-panel.sh` no longer flags git-ignored side files (uv.lock, .venv). Neither is in the frozen file list; they change no review content.
