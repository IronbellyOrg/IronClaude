## 3. The attack questions, answered

**Does the dangling clause bias Z0 and K (confuse, add a ledger, change finding counts)?** Unknown. No real-model run of a Z0 or K brief with the clause exists; the Recipe Ledger rehearsals use fake models (0.028 s, 108 output tokens). Possible effects, all UNVERIFIED: a phantom `## Ledger` (the only one the count sees); a clause-induced format failure scored as zero findings (R-I, skipped by the count); a slower Z0, which loosens the D1 cap and lowers the spike's cost per item (R-A, probe INV-011); a checklist interaction in K (R-B, no mechanism found). The clause is one phrase in a four-sentence rule whose other three sentences are identical in every arm.

**Does verbatim keep the arms comparable?** Yes. CB-2 is the same bytes in all three arms, so SS minus Z0 is the ledger rule plus the sheet plus the clause becoming operative, and K minus Z0 is the K paragraph only. The two workspaces also stay byte-identical (Recipe Ledger `DESIGN.md` DR-12 and DR-15, Tier A copy rule at line 184), which keeps their combined Z0 and K schedule possible (it saves 132 panel runs).

**Would a reworded CB-2 for all arms break REQ-05 and REQ-23?** REQ-05, yes as written: `tests/test_briefs.py::test_cb_verbatim_against_mr` compares each `cbN.txt` byte for byte with the brainstorm's quoted line, and the brainstorm may not be edited, so the test would need a registered exception. REQ-23, no: one reworded text used in every arm keeps the empty-sheet SS brief equal to Z0's by hash. A reword has three further costs: dropping the clause removes the only instruction that every PROBLEM row gets a findings line (`briefs/ledger-rule.txt` and `sheet-block.txt` do not say it; without it the row is promoted as MINOR, non-blocking, `DESIGN.md` 511-519, so SS would lose blocking findings); moving that duty to an SS-only part after the ledger rule changes the order and is untested; and CB-3 ("A tool line or a recipe step") and CB-5 ("The checks you were given") also refer to parts Z0 lacks, so a CB-2-only reword leaves Z0 not clean. The Recipe Ledger copy must change too (outside this task).

**What is the measurable harm, and how large?**
- The count sees an exact `## Ledger` line in finished reviewers' reports. K: 60 reports (4 documents x 5 runs x 3 reviewers). Zero seen bounds the rate at 4.87% (95%, one-sided). The chance of seeing at least one heading in 60 reports is 0.70 at a true 2%, 0.95 at 5%, 0.998 at 10%.
- Z0 on the primary panel: at most 237 reports (20 runs in Stage 1, 59 in Stage 2), bound 1.26% at zero; 291 with the haiku and sonnet panels (1.02%). Stage 2 runs only for arms that pass D1, D2 or D3. The evidence pack said 591 and 0.51%; that was an error (`adversarial/work/corrections.md` item 1).
- Effect on found fraction, finding counts, time and format failures: not measured by any option and UNVERIFIED. The only number offered, a 0.001 to 0.003 shift in K's mean, rests on a guessed 0.05 loss per affected report. For scale, D2 needs +0.12 (lower bound above +0.04) and D7 needs +0.04 over K.

**Is there a better option?** One that is better as advice, none that is better as a replacement. (d) verbatim plus a written rule for the count fixes the only real gap, costs nothing in tests or hashes, and keeps both workspaces identical. The per-arm variant (c: full clause only in SS with a sheet, clause-free in Z0, K and empty-sheet SS, two hashes recorded) keeps REQ-23 but breaks REQ-05 for Z0 and K, adds a text difference between SS and Z0 beyond the ledger machinery that no model has seen, needs a new part, a registry entry, four or more test changes, and breaks the Recipe Ledger rule that its brief starts with exactly the Z0 brief (its `DESIGN.md` DR-2). (b) one reworded text for all arms: see the third paragraph above.

## 4. Non-blocking notes

- **N1. "Owner decision if above 0" has no content, and after the freeze it has no cheap action.** A CB-2 change after step 14.5 fails `bin/check-order.sh freeze` (line 81: the freeze tag must precede the hold-out ground-truth commit; line 88: no frozen file may change; `freeze.sh` line 43 hashes `briefs/*.txt`). It would need a new tag and a re-commit and re-attestation of the hold-out ground truth (`TESTING.md` B5 to B8, lines 223-227): no model run, but not edits only. The window to decide CB-2 is step 14.3.
- **N2. The count is narrow.** It sees one spelling (an exact `## Ledger` line, `analyze.py` 1024), skips reviewers that did not finish ok (1018), groups by arm and not by panel (so the Z0 count mixes the primary, haiku and sonnet panels), prints "0 of 0" if every reviewer of an arm is invalid, and its test is synthetic (`test_report.py` 326-338). It cannot see effects on found fraction, finding counts, time or format failures. Zero must never be written as "harmless".
- **N3. The dangling references are wider than CB-2.** CB-3 refers to a tool line or recipe step that Z0 and K lack; CB-5 refers to checks that Z0 lacks (K's paragraph names eight). Only the CB-2 heading is counted.
- **N4. Real-model evidence comes late and is thin.** The first Z0 reports with the clause come from the timing spike in step 15, after the freeze: about 5 Z0 panel runs (about 15 reports, UNVERIFIED how many are kept; `run-spike.sh` 79 per the debate). Chance of seeing a heading in 15 reports: 0.26 at 2%, 0.54 at 5%, 0.79 at 10%. It cannot show K, finding counts or found fraction. `run-panel.sh` accepts a null freeze tag, so the owner could order the spike before 14.3 if a real look is wanted first.
- **N5. Direction of any bias is unknown.** Do not write that a damaged control "flatters SS": an inflated K would make D7 conservative, an invalid Z0 reviewer scored as zero (DD-8) would flatter SS on D2, and `invalid` is computed (`analyze.py` 391) but not printed in the arm table (1157-1165).
- **N6. The K lead-in is still a draft** (`briefs/k-lead.txt` line 1, owner-decisions line 17). Decide it with CB-2 at step 14.3; a later K edit would reopen the K and clause interaction (R-B). `freeze.sh` does not check the draft marker.
- **N7. `results/copy-briefs.py` rewrites every brief from the brainstorm unconditionally** (lines 29-36, 66-68). Harmless while CB-2 is verbatim; it would overwrite a reword.
- **N8. The Recipe Ledger workspace adopts this decision** (its DR-15, owner row 6 at line 823). Any later reword is made once, before 14.5, in both workspaces.
- **N9. Recommended hardening text (advice only; a PASS changes no file).** If the owner wants the registration closed, append to the DD-7 row and to 15.1 row 3: "(a) The heading count is descriptive. Report 'n of N reports' with the exact one-sided 95% upper bound (K: 4.87% at 0 of 60; Z0 on the primary panel: about 1.3% at 0 of at most 237). It sees only an exact `## Ledger` line, skips reviewers that did not finish ok, groups by arm and not by panel, and cannot see effects on found fraction, finding counts, time or format failures; zero does not show the clause is harmless. CB-3 and CB-5 also refer to parts Z0 lacks and stay verbatim for the same reason. (b) CB-2 is decided at step 14.3. After step 14.5 a change to `cb2.txt` is a new freeze (a new tag and a re-commit of the hold-out ground truth); the count is a reported limitation only and may not change a gate or discount D2 or D7."
